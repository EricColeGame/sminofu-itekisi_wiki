## How to Get the Most From a First Listen

An hour is a real commitment, so it helps to have a plan. The approach below splits the record into manageable passes without breaking the intended order.

| Pass | What to play | Why it works |
|---|---|---|

A few practical tips:

- **Use good headphones for the intro.** "Benilindele Bani" is only 1:28, but it is the setup for the title track and easy to skip by accident.
- **Do not shuffle the first half.** The intro-to-title-track handoff is the single best sequencing choice on the record.
- **Give the long tracks a real window.** "Igeja" at 6:05 and "Iyeke Leyondaba" at 5:11 need attention; they lose the most when played in the background.
- **Note which guest track lands hardest for you.** With only four features, individual preferences tend to be strong and specific.
- **Save the closer for last.** "eMachobeni" works because it follows "Iyeke Leyondaba," not because it stands alone.

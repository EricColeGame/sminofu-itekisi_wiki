## Why Release Dates Get Confused: Announcement, Drop, and Rollout

Most confusion around the **Sminofu Itekisi album release date** comes from mixing up four different dates that all sound like "the release date." They are not the same, and they rarely fall on the same day.

An **announcement date** is when the artist or page first says the project is coming. A **pre-save date** is when links go live so fans can queue the album in advance. The **street date** is the official release day recorded in distribution metadata. The **platform live date** is when the audio actually becomes playable in your country, which can lag by hours or occasionally by a day.

| Term | What it actually means | Date it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement | First public mention of the project | Not the release date |
| Pre-save / pre-order | Links open to queue the album | Usually days or weeks early |
| Street date | Official date in distributor metadata | The authoritative date |
| Platform live date | Audio playable in your region | Can differ by time zone |
| Deluxe / reissue | Expanded edition later | A separate, later date |

Two more wrinkles matter. First, midnight releases roll out by time zone, so a fan in Johannesburg may hear *iTekisi* hours before a fan in London. Second, catalog metadata sometimes gets corrected after launch, which means an early listing may show a placeholder date that later changes. If you screenshot a date on day one and it looks odd a week later, you are not imagining things.

## Reading Maskandi Lyrics With the Right Context

Maskandi is a Zulu genre built on storytelling, and its lyrics often carry more social commentary than a casual listen suggests. A few conventions are worth knowing before you judge any transcription of the Sminofu Itekisi lyrics.

First, the narrator is a character. Maskandi singers frequently adopt a persona — the braggart, the traveler, the observer — and lines spoken in that voice are not the artist's personal opinion. Second, humor is structural. Teasing and exaggeration are how the genre handles tension. Third, repetition is deliberate. Repeated lines are not filler; they are how a crowd joins in.

Listeners who come from pop or hip-hop often expect a verse-hook-verse-hook structure and a clear message. Maskandi is looser. It circles a subject, returns to a phrase, and lets the audience fill gaps. Once you accept that shape, the song opens up.

The artist's profile also places the record in good company: listeners of Sminofu frequently also play uGatsheni, Udumakahle, Mjikelo, Thokozani Langa, and Mjabulisi, all of whom work in the same tradition. If you enjoy the conversational tone of *iTekisi*, that list is a natural next step.

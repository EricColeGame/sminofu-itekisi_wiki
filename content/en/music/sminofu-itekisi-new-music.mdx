## How to Get the Most Out of the Album

Seventeen tracks is a lot to absorb in one sitting, and streaming habits encourage skipping. A few practical approaches make the record land better.

**Listen in two passes.** Take tracks 1 through 9 first, then 10 through 17. The album has a natural midpoint around "Emabhilidini," and splitting there keeps the guest features from blurring together.

**Use headphones for the guitar work.** Maskandi is built on interlocking acoustic lines, and phone speakers flatten exactly the detail that makes the genre distinctive.

**Read along with the titles.** Several song names — "Umuntu Ongenacala" ("a person without blame"), "Imizamo" ("efforts"), "Umalumekazi" (aunt) — point directly at the subject matter. Knowing what a title means changes how a verse reads, even if you do not speak isiZulu fluently.

**Build a short playlist first.** Pull five tracks, live with them for a week, then return for the rest. Player experience in long-running genre communities consistently shows that albums absorbed slowly stay in rotation longer than albums binge-heard once.

## Kungani Ingoma Yesishiyagalolunye Ifanele Ukubhekwa Eduze

Uma ubulokhu uphinda i-*iTekisi* kaSminofu kusukela ngosuku lokukhishwa, kungenzeka ukuthi ingoma yesishiyagalolunye yakwenza wama futhi wathatha ifoni yakho. Ukusesha **amagama engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini** kuye kwakhula ngokuqhubekayo kusukela i-albhamu yakhishwa ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, futhi kulula ukuzwa ukuthi kungani: ingoma esekelwa u-Ojakalasi ifaka indaba eningi emizuzwini emithathu namasekhondi angamashumi amabili. Inkinga ukuthi ukuthola **amagama engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini** ethembekile kudinga umzamo omkhulu kunosesho olulodwa, ngoba umbhalo ogcwele wengoma awukashicilelwa ngokusemthethweni ndawo.

Lelo gebe phakathi kwesidingo nokuhlinzekwa yilokho kanye lo mhlahlandlela okubhekana nakho. Esikhundleni sokunamathisela imigqa esezwakale kancane ethathwe ezindaweni zokuphawula, sizodlula kokunye okuqinisekisiwe ngale ngoma, ukuthi ingena kanjani ku-albhamu enezingoma eziyishumi nesikhombisa, kanye nezindlela ezethembekile kakhulu zokuthola amagama engoma anembile ngaphandle kokuqagela.

### Isihloko Sikhombisani

Izihloko zezingoma ze-albhamu zibhalwe ngesiZulu, futhi *iTekisi* ngokwayo ikhomba ematekisini amancane ahambisa izigidi zabantu baseNingizimu Afrika nsuku zonke — isithombe esicebile, sasezinsukwini zansuku zonke esiqinisa irekhodi. *Emabhilidini* lifundeka njengesimo sendawo esihlobene nezakhiwo noma izindlu ezingamabhulokhi, okuyindlela abalaleli abaningi abayihumushe ngayo ezingxoxweni zomphakathi. Leyo ndlela yokufunda ihambisana nomkhuba we-albhamu wokusekela izindaba zayo ezindaweni ezibonakalayo: iziteshi zamatekisi, imigwaqo, amakhaya omndeni, kanye namakhona agcwele empilweni yansuku zonke.

Kuthathe lokho njengokuhumusha hhayi njengeqiniso. Ngaphandle kweshidi lamagama engoma elisemthethweni, incazelo eqondile yesihloko kumongo iseyinto abalandeli abasahlanganisa ngayo.

## Okuhlangenwe Nakho Komlaleli: Abalaleli Bathini

Ezingxoxweni zabalandeli kusukela ngesonto lokukhishwa, kuvela izindikimba ezimbalwa ezihambisanayo. **Okuhlangenwe nakho komlaleli** kusikisela ukuthi *Emabhilidini* isebenza kahle kakhulu njengendawo yokuphumula phakathi kwe-albhamu — ilula emoyeni kune-"Igeja" yemizuzu eyisithupha eyandulela ngezingoma ezine, kodwa isagcina umucu wendaba werekhodi. **Imibiko yomphakathi** iphinde ibone ukuthi ivesi lika-Ojakalasi iyingxenye abantu abayidlala kaningi, futhi okuyiyona abayifuna kakhulu ukuthi ibhalwe.

Kukhona ukukhungatheka okuphindaphindayo okufanele kuphawulwe: abalaleli abaningana baye bahlangabezana namakhasi amagama engoma ahlanganisa i-*Emabhilidini* ne-"eMachobeni," ingoma yokugcina ye-albhamu. Lezi zihloko ezimbili zibukeka zifana uma zibukwa ngokushesha, futhi okungenani amakhasi ambalwa eziqoqi azididanisile. Uma isiza samagama engoma sibala i-*Emabhilidini* njengengoma yokugcina, lokho kuwuphawu oluyisixwayiso mayelana nokuqukethwe kwaso okunye.

## Ukufaka Ingoma Esithombeni Esikhulu

* iTekisi* ukukhishwa okubalulekile: izingoma eziyishumi nesikhombisa nomculo ongaphezulu kwehora, okhishwe ngaphansi kophawu lukaSminofu uqobo kanye ne-EMPIRE. Kulelo khathalogi, *Emabhilidini* isebenza njengesixhumanisi — ingoma ehlanganyelwe, enesivinini esimaphakathi, egcina umfutho phakathi kwezingxenye ezinkulu ze-albhamu. Yingakho kanye isidingo **samagama engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini** siqhubeka: iyingoma abantu abafuna ukuyicula kanye nayo, hhayi nje ukuyidlala ngemuva.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe amagama engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini asemthethweni ayatholakala noma kuphi?**
Akakatholakali okwamanje. Kusukela ngasekupheleni kuka-Okthoba 2026, asikho ishidi lamagama engoma elisemthethweni elishicilelwe lale ngoma. Noma yikuphi ukubhalwa okuphelele okuthola ku-inthanethi kwenziwe abalandeli, ngakho kuhlola ngokuqhathanisa nomsindo ngaphambi kokukucaphuna.

**Ingabe i-Emabhilidini iyingoma yokugcina ku-iTekisi?**
Cha — lokho ukudidanisa okuvamile. Ingoma yokugcina yi-"eMachobeni." Uma ikhasi lamagama engoma libiza i-*Emabhilidini* njengengoma yeshumi nesikhombisa, eminye imininingwane yalo cishe ayithembekile futhi.

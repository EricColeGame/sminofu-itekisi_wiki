## Kungani i-"Iyeke Leyondaba" Ifanele Ilalelwe Ngokucophelela

Uma ubufuna **amazwi engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Iyeke Leyondaba**, awuwedwa — le ngoma iyinombolo 16 kuphrojekthi enezingoma eziyi-17, ithatha u-5:11, futhi ivala i-albhamu ngomuzwa obukeka sengathi ushiya into ingaqediwe ngamabomu.

Lokho kuhlelwa kubalulekile. Ku-albhamu eyakhiwe yazungeza ukuhamba, ukusebenza kanzima, kanye nohambo lwansuku zonke, i-"Iyeke Leyondaba" iyisikhathi lapho ijubane lehla khona futhi umxoxi ebheka emuva. Ukuthola **amazwi engoma ethi Sminofu Itekisi Iyeke Leyondaba** ngokuphelele akulula njengoba kuzwakala, ngoba ingoma isanda kukhishwa futhi imibhalo esemthethweni ayikakasakazwa kumadathabhezi ajwayelekile amazwi engoma. Le mhlahlandlela ivala isikhala: lapho ingoma itholakala khona ku-albhamu, ukuthi isihloko sesiZulu siphakamisa ini, nokuthi ungawalandela kanjani wonke umugqa ngokuqiniseka esikhundleni sokuqagela.

"Emabhilidini" iyawahlula lawo mkhuba ngoba yenza izinto ezintathu ngesikhathi esisodwa: imfushane ngokwanele ukuba iphindwe, inikela imakrofoni kusivakashi, futhi ifika ngqo lapho amandla e-albhamu adinga ukuphakanyiswa.

## Indlela i-Emabhilidini Ehambisana Ngayo Nokulandelana Kwe-Albhamu

Ukulandelana yikho lapho ama-albhamu amade anqobwa noma ehlulwa khona, futhi **iTekisi** ihlelwe ngenhloso ecacile. Isingeniso sifushane — imizuzu engu-88 — futhi sidlulisela ngokushesha engomeni eyinhloko yemizuzu emine. Kusukela lapho, uSminofu ushintsha phakathi kwezingoma ezicabangisayo nalezo ezigxile esigqini, futhi izindawo zezivakashi zihlukaniswe cishe izingoma ezimbili kuya kwezintathu kunokuba ziqoqwe ndawonye.

| Isigaba | Izingoma | Umsebenzi |
|---|---|---|
| Ukuqala | 1–2 | Isingeniso nengoma eyinhloko kusungula ithoni |
| Ingxenye yokuqala | 3–6 | Izinto zodwa zakha ububanzi be-albhamu |
| Iqembu lokuqala lezivakashi | 7–9 | Ukusebenzisana kwezivakashi, i-Emabhilidini ivala |
| Ingxenye ephakathi | 10–14 | Uhambo olude kakhulu lodwa, luphela ngezivakashi ezimbili |
| Ingxenye yokugcina yesithathu | 15–17 | Ukubuya kwezivakashi, ukuvala okunwetshiwe, ukuvalelisa okufushane |

Njengoba ibekwe njengengoma yokugcina yalelo qembu lokuqala lezivakashi, i-"Emabhilidini" isebenza njengendlela yokukhulula ukushuba. Ingoma eyandulelayo, "Madagascar," ithatha u-4:20 futhi iyodwa ngokuphelele; ingoma elandelayo, "Es'thenjini," ithatha u-4:12 futhi nayo iyodwa. I-duet esheshayo ka-3:20 phakathi kwezingoma ezimbili ezinde zodwa inikeza indlebe ushintsho lwendlela yokuzwa ngesikhathi esifanele — umininingwane okufanele uwuqaphele ngesikhathi esizayo uma uzwa i-Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini isezimweni sayo.

Kubakhi bamalisti okudlalayo, lokho futhi kusho ukuthi ingoma ihlezi kahle ekujikelezweni okufushane. Iyingoma eyodwa eqinile, kodwa ilahlekelwa umthelela omncane uma ihlukaniswa nokulandelana kwezingoma 7 kuya ku-10.

- **Nikeza isethulo ithuba.** Ngamasekendi angu-1:28 simfushane ngokwanele ukuthi singagcinwa, futhi sibeka ingoma eyisihloko kahle kunokuqala ngqo engomeni yesibili.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe ingoma ka-Sminofu ethi iTekisi iyafana ne-albhamu?**
Yebo futhi cha. *iTekisi* yigama lazo zombili i-albhamu enezingoma ezingu-17 eyakhishwa ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, nengoma yayo yesibili. Ingoma ithatha u-4:06 futhi iwumsebenzi ka-Sminofu eyedwa, kanti i-albhamu iyonke ihlanganisa izingoma ezinhlanu ezinabaculi abavakashile.

**Ubani ovakashile engomeni ka-Sminofu ethi iTekisi?**
Akekho. Ingoma eyisihloko yenziwa ngokuphelele ngu-Sminofu. Abavakashi be-albhamu bangabakwa-Londeka Shangase (kabili), Ojakalasi, Njabulo Langa, noZanefa Ngidi, bonke kwezinye izingoma.

**I-albhamu ephelele ye-iTekisi ithatha isikhathi esingakanani?**
Iqukethe izingoma ezingu-17 futhi ithatha cishe ihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6, okusho isilinganiso esicishe sibe imizuzu engu-3 nemizuzwana engu-55 ingoma ngayinye. Ingoma emfushane kakhulu isethulo esingamasekendi angu-1:28; ende kunazo zonke yi-"Igeja" enamasekendi angu-6:05.

**Ngingayisakaza kuphi ingoma ka-Sminofu ethi iTekisi?**

## Kungani Lesi Sethulo Semizuzwana Engu-88 Kufanele Silalelwe Sonke

Zimbalwa izingoma zokuvula i-albhamu ezithwala isisindo esingako esikhathini esingakanani njenge-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani, isethulo semizuzu engu-1:28 esiqala i-albhamu kaSminofu yango-2026, *iTekisi*. Uma ufike lapha ngemva kokuzwa leyo ngoma yokuvula futhi ufuna umongo, usendaweni efanele: lo mhlahlandlela ukhuluma ngokuthi iyini le ngoma, ikuphi lapho ihlezi khona ohlwini lwezingoma ezingu-17, nokuthi kungani ucezu olufushane kangaka lubalulekile. Izethulo yizingoma ezilula ukuzidlula — yikho kanye okwenza i-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani ifanele ukulalelwa ngokucophelela.

Ukuvula kuyisithembiso. Kukutshela ukuthi hlobo luni lweculo osuzochitha ihora nalo, futhi kuphrojekthi ende njenge-*iTekisi* (izingoma ezingu-17, cishe imizuzu engu-66), leyo mizuzwana yokuqala engu-88 yenza umsebenzi omangazayo.

## Kusho Ukuthini "Benilindele Bani"?

Isihloko singesiZulu, futhi ukusihlukanisa izingxenye kuyasiza. Umsuka *-lind-* uthwala umqondo wokulinda noma wokubheka okuthile, kuyilapho *bani* kuyigama lombuzo elithi "ubani." Uma kuhlangene, le nkulumo ivame ukuqondwa ngabalaleli njengombuzo othi "Ulindele bani?"

| Isakhi | Esikufakayo |
|---|---|
| Benilindele | Yakhiwe emsukeni wokulinda, enophawu lomuntu wesibili ebuningini — cishe "nilindele" |
| Bani | "Ubani" — kuguqula isitatimende sibe umbuzo |
| (Intro) | Kukhombisa umsebenzi wengoma njengesethulo hhayi iculo elizimele |
| iTekisi | Isihloko se-albhamu; igama lesiZulu elisho itekisi elincane |

Leyo ndlela yokufunda ibalulekile ngoba ishintsha indlela ozwa ngayo yonke enye ingoma. I-albhamu evula ngokubuza ukuthi ulindele bani, kuphrojekthi eqanjwe ngokuhamba komphakathi, ikumema ukuba ucabange ngendawo oya kuyo, ukubambezeleka, nokuthi ubani ohamba nawe. Lokhu kuwukuhumusha hhayi isitatimende esisemthethweni senhloso — akukho humusho eshicilelwe ehambisana nokukhishwa — kodwa kuyinto evumelana nabalaleli abakhuluma nge-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ubani ocula i-"Benilindele Bani"?**
USminofu ucula le ngoma eyedwa. Iyona ngoma yokuqala ku-*iTekisi* futhi ukuphela kwengoma ku-albhamu engenaye umculi ovakashile obhalwe.

**I-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani inde kangakanani?**
Ithatha u-1:28, okuyenza ibe yingoma emfushane kunazo zonke ku-albhamu ngokwesikhala esikhulu — elandelayo emfushane, "Why Umehlulela," ingu-3:12.

**Isihloko sisho ukuthini ngesiNgisi?**
Le nkulumo ivame ukuqondwa ngokuthi "Ulindele bani?" Ihlanganisa umsuka wesenzo sesiZulu ohambisana nokulinda negama lombuzo *bani*. Akukho humusho esemthethweni eshicilelwe, ngakho lokhu kusalokhu kuwukuhumusha.

**Ingabe i-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani iyatholakala njengeculo elizimele?**
Akukho siqinisekiso sokukhishwa kweculo elizimele ezintweni ezitholakalayo. Okwamanje, ingoma ingcono ukuyizwa njengengoma yokuqala ye-*iTekisi*, ekhishwe ngo-16 Okthoba 2026.

**Kungani isethulo semizuzwana engu-88 sibalulekile?**
Ngoba iyinto yokuqala umlaleli ayizwayo ku-albhamu enezingoma ezingu-17. Isungula umbuzo oyinhloko wesihloko, idlulisela ngqo engomeni enesihloko, futhi ikubiza ngaphansi kwemizuzu eyodwa nesigamu — ukutshala okuncane ukuze uqonde kahle ukuthi yini eyenziwa yiculo eliphelele.

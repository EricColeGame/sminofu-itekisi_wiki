## Sminofu Itekisi Rush Hour Kuchazwa: Ukukhishwa Okubili, Ukusesha Okukodwa

Nawu umbono omfushane. I-*iTekisi* iphrojekthi entsha: izingoma eziyi-17, imizuzu engu-66, yakhishwa ngo-16 Okthoba 2026, futhi amalungelo okushicilela aphethwe yi-Sminofu / EMPIRE. I-*Rush Hour* isendaweni yangaphambili kuyo leyo discography, inonyaka ongu-2024 kuphrofayela esemthethweni yomculi uSminofu. Lezi zihloko ezimbili zihlangana ndawonye ekusesheni ngoba abalaleli abathola esisodwa ngokushesha bafuna esinye, futhi izinkundla zokusakaza zizibala zihambisana ngaphansi kwegama elilodwa.

Lokho kuhlangana kunomphumela ongokoqobo. Uma uvula ukukhishwa okungelona ulindele okunye, ukulalela kwakho kokuqala kuchithwa ulindele izingoma ezazingasoze zivele. Itafula elingezansi lihlukanisa kokubili ngaphambi kokuthi ucindezele ukudlala.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe i-Sminofu Itekisi Rush Hour i-albhamu eyodwa noma ezimbili?**
Kukhishwa okubili okuhlukene. *iTekisi* i-albhamu enezingoma eziyi-17 yango-16 Okthoba 2026, kanti *Rush Hour* iphrojekthi ka-Sminofu yangaphambili yango-2024. Le nkulumo ehlanganisiwe iwumkhuba wokusesha, hhayi isihloko esisemthethweni.

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-iTekisi?**
Izingoma eziyishumi nesikhombisa ezithatha ihora eli-1 nemizuzu engu-6, ngobude obujwayelekile bengoma obucishe bube ngu-3:55. Eyemfushane kakhulu isethulo esingumzuzu ongu-1:28; ende kunazo zonke ngu-"Igeja" onemizuzu engu-6:05.

**Ubani oqavile ku-iTekisi?**

**Yini okufanele umlaleli omusha ayidlale kuqala?**

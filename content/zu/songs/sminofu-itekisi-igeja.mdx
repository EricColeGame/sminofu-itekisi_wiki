## Igeja: Kungani Iculo Lemizuzu Eyisithupha Ligxilisa i-Sminofu Itekisi Igeja

"Igeja" iyiculo lesihlanu, ithatha u-6:05, futhi iyiculo elide kakhulu kunazo zonke ku-albhamu — imizuzwana engu-54 ngaphezu kwesibili ngobude. Lokho kuphela kungayenza ibe ephawulekayo, kodwa indawo elibekwe kuyo yenza umsebenzi omkhulu kunobude baso. Ngesikhathi lifika, usuke wezwa isingeniso sika-1:28, iculo lesihloko lemizuzu emine, "Amalengiso," kanye no-"Why Umehlulela." I-albhamu isivele yazinzisa ijubane layo. Bese u-"Igeja" wehlisa ijubane lazo zonke izinto.

Izingoma ezinde aziyona into engavamile ku-Maskandi. Amalungiselelo alo hlobo aholelwa isiginci ngokwesiko ashiya isikhala sezingeniso ezinde, izingxenye ezikhulunywayo, nemisho yezwi ephindaphindiwe eyakha ukushuba kunokugijimela ekhorasi. Ngakho iculo lemizuzu eyisithupha akuyona isitatimende sokuvelela kodwa ukubuyela esimweni esijwayelekile — uhlobo lweculo oluvuzela umlaleli ongadluli ngokushesha.

Kunoma ubani ofika ngokusesha i-**Sminofu Itekisi Igeja**, isifundo esingokoqobo silula: yilona culo okufanele uhlole i-albhamu ngalo. Uma isakhiwo semizuzu eyisithupha sigcina ukunaka kwakho, nayo yonke i-albhamu izokwenza njalo. Uma uthanda izingoma eziqinile, eziqala ngezingxenye ezikhanga, qala ngeculo lesihloko noma "Amalengiso" bese uqhubeka kancane kancane.

Imibiko yomphakathi mayelana nezingoma ezinde ze-Maskandi ivamise ukusho iphuzu elifanayo — lezi zingoma azivamile ukuziveza ngokudlala kanye. Isingeniso, ukushintsha phakathi kwengoma, nokuphindaphinda kokugcina kuzwakala ngendlela ehlukile ekulaleleni kwesibili noma kwesithathu.

**Ungubani uSminofu, futhi yini i-iTekisi?**

**Lide kangakanani i-"Igeja," futhi livela kuphi ku-albhamu?**
I-"Igeja" iyiculo lesihlanu futhi ithatha u-6:05, okuyenza ibe iculo elide kakhulu ku-albhamu. Yilona culo elihlotshaniswa kakhulu nokuseshwa kwe-**Sminofu Itekisi Igeja**, futhi lisebenza njengendawo yokuqala ephezulu ye-albhamu ngaphambi kokuba kuqale isigaba sezivakashi.

**Ingabe i-iTekisi inezingoma ezihambisana nezivakashi?**

**Ngingayilalela kuphi i-Sminofu Itekisi Igeja?**

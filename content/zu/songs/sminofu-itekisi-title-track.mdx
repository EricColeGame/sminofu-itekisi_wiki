## Kungani Ingoma Eyinhloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi Ibalulekile

Izingoma eziyizinhloko zama-albhamu zinomsebenzi othile: zikhangisa ukuthi i-albhamu ikhuluma ngani ngaphambi kokuthi umlaleli anikele ngehora lakhe. Ku-*iTekisi*, lowo msebenzi uwela kwingoma eyinhloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi — ingoma yesibili kuphrojekthi enezingoma eziyi-17 eyafika ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, nge-Sminofu ne-EMPIRE. Ithatha imizuzu emine nemizuzwana eyisithupha, ayinaye umculi oyisivakashi, futhi ihlezi ngqo ngemuva kwesethulo esiyimizuzu engu-1:28. Lokho kubekwa kwakho kunomqondo. Isethulo siyazilungiselela; ingoma eyinhloko iwumbono wokuqala wangempela.

Uma ubukade uzama ukuthola ukuthi ingoma eyinhloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi iyini ngempela, ukuthi ihlangana kuphi ohlelweni lwamathrekhi, nokuthi i-albhamu eyizungezile ikufanele yini ukuchitha ubusuku bakho, lo mhlahlandlela uhlanganisa uhlu lwamathrekhi, umongo, namathiphu ambalwa asebenzayo okulalela.

Ngamafuphi: iyisisekelo se-albhamu ende, ingenye yezinto ezicula yedwa ezinzulu kakhulu kule albhamu, futhi iyindawo engcono kakhulu yokuqala uma ufuna ukuqonda ukuthi i-*iTekisi* yenzani ngaphambi kokuthi ulalela yonke imizuzu engu-66.

## Okufanele Ukulalele Kwingoma Eyinhloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi

### Isihloko ngokwaso

*iTekisi* igama lesiZulu elisho "iteksi," futhi i-albhamu isebenzisa leso sakhiwo emagameni ayo. Leso yisithombe esicebile kumongo waseNingizimu Afrika — ukuhamba ndawonye, ukuhamba nsuku zonke, izingxoxo phakathi kwabantu ongabazi — futhi kunikeza ingoma eyinhloko ingqikithi eyakhelwe ngaphakathi. Noma ingoma isebenzisa isingathekiso ngokoqobo noma isithatha njengomuzwa, ukwazi ukuhunyushwa kushintsha indlela amazwi azwakala ngayo.

### Ulimi nendlela yokucula

Uhlu lwamathrekhi lwakhiwe cishe ngokuphelele ngezihloko zesiZulu, futhi ingoma eyinhloko ilandela lelo phethini. Uma ungakhulumi isiZulu, ungakuvumeli lokho kukuvimbe: umculo nesigqi kuthwala ingxenye enkulu yencazelo, futhi ukuhunyushwa kwamazwi kuyatholakala kabanzi kumapulatifomu okusakaza nakwimiphakathi yabalandeli. Ukulalela okwesibili noma okwesithathu kunokuhunyushwa okuvuliwe kuyasiza ngempela.

### Ubude nesivinini

Ku-4:06, ingoma inethuba lesethulo esifanele, okungenani izingxenye ezimbili ezigcwele zokucula, nesiphetho esingezwakali siphoqiwe. Qhathanisa nalokho ne-"Why Umehlulela" ku-3:12, eyakhelwe ukunikeza amandla ashesha, futhi uzwa umehluko wamabhongo phakathi kwengoma eyinhloko nethrekhi ejulile.

### Uhlu lokulalela olusebenzayo

- **Lalela kanye ngaphandle kokuphazanyiswa.** Ungaskroli, ungadluli. Ingoma ivuza ukunaka kunokulalela kwangemuva.
- **Lalela okwesibili ngamazwi noma ngokuhunyushwa.** Incazelo ifika sekwephuzile emculweni omningi wesiZulu uma ungumkhulumi onolwazi.
- **Yidlale ngokulandelana kwe-albhamu.** Ukudlula kwesethulo esingu-1:28 kuya kwingoma eyinhloko kuyingxenye yokuhlangenwe nakho.
- **Yiqhathanise nethrekhi yesithathu.** "Amalengiso" ku-4:10 icishe ifane ngobude, okwenza laba ababili babe yisifundo esiwusizo sokuthi uSminofu ushintsha kanjani umoya ngesabelo esifanayo sesivinini.
- **Bese ugxumela kuzivakashi.** Amathrekhi 7, 9, 12, no-15 akhombisa uhlangothi oluhlukile lwe-albhamu.

**I-albhamu yaphuma nini?**

*iTekisi* yakhishwa ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, nge-Sminofu ne-EMPIRE. Inezingoma eziyi-17 futhi ithatha ihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6 isiyonke.

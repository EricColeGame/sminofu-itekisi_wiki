## Kungani i-Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele Isohlwini Lokudlala Lwabo Bonke Abalandeli Be-Maskandi

Abalaleli be-Maskandi abasesha **Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele** balandela okunye kokukhishwe okukhulunywa kakhulu kukhalenda yomculo waseNingizimu Afrika. I-albhamu kaSminofu ethi *iTekisi* yafika ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, futhi zingakapheli izinsuku umusho **Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele** waba indlela ejwayelekile abalandeli ababhalela ngayo befuna le albhamu — ingxenye isihloko se-albhamu, ingxenye umugqa wengoma okhunjulwayo, ingxenye ilukuluku nje. Kungani lokho kubalulekile? Ngoba *iTekisi* akuyona i-single esheshayo nje. Kuyisitatimende esigcwele: izingoma ezingu-17, cishe ihora nemizuzu eyisithupha yokuxoxa indaba kwe-Maskandi, ekhishwe nge-EMPIRE.

Leso silinganiso asijwayelekile. Ukukhishwa okuningi okuzimele kwe-Maskandi kuza nezingoma eziyisishiyagalombili kuya kweziyishumi nambili; iphrojekthi enezingoma ezingu-17 ikhombisa umculi onokuthile afuna ukukusho nelebuli ekulungele ukumseka. Kubalandeli besikhathi eside balolu hlobo lomculo nakubaqalayo abafike kule albhamu ngesiqeshana esifushane, le albhamu inikeza ingxube engavamile ye-Maskandi yendabuko eqhutshwa ukugeja nesiphetho sesimanje esilungele umsakazo.

Lo mhlahlandlela uchaza okushiwo yi-*iTekisi* ngempela, ukuthi yiziphi izingoma okufanele uzilalele kuqala, ubani ovela njengesivakashi, nendlela yokuthola okuningi kule albhamu ekulaleleni kwakho kokuqala. Uma ufike lapha ngokubhala i-Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele kubha yokusesha, thatha lokhu njengolwazi oluphelele lokuqondisa.

## Umsindo: Izimpande ze-Maskandi Ngokuqedwa Kwesimanje

I-Maskandi iwuhlobo lomculo olwakhiwe ngemigqa yeziginci ehlanganyelayo, ukuhlabela ngokuphendulana, namazwi asebenza njengokugxeka umphakathi kanye nokujabulisa. *iTekisi* incike kulelo siko ngaphandle kokuliphatha njengento yasemnyuziyamu. Ukukhiqizwa, okwenziwe ngokwesekwa yi-EMPIRE, kugcina iziginci ziphambili emsindweni kuyilapho kunikeza umsindo ophansi nezigubhu indawo eyanele yokuzwakala kuzipikha zocingo — okuyindawo lapho, kwabalaleli abaningi baseNingizimu Afrika, i-albhamu ehlulelwa khona ngempela.

Kukhona amaphethini ambalwa avelayo kuzo zonke izingoma ezingu-17:

- **Ukuxoxa indaba kuqala.** Izingoma ezifana no-"Umuntu Ongenacala" no-"Imizamo" zakhela emavesini axoxayo kunokuba zibe nehook eyodwa ephindwayo, okunomvuzo uma ulalela ngokunaka.
- **Ukwehluka kwesivinini.** I-albhamu ishintsha phakathi kwezingoma ezisheshayo, ezidansekayo nezingxenye ezihamba kancane, ezicabangayo, ukuze ukulalela okuthatha ihora kungabi yinto eyodwa eqhubekayo.
- **Ulimi nokushintsha kwezilimi.** Izihloko ezifana no-"Why Umehlulela" zibonisa uSminofu ehlanganisa amagama esiZulu nesiNgisi, umkhuba ojwayelekile futhi ogqama kakhulu ku-Maskandi yesimanje.
- **Indawo njengengqikithi.** I-"Madagascar," "Es'thenjini," ne-"eMachobeni" zonke zikhomba ezindaweni, indlela yakudala ye-Maskandi yokugxilisa ingoma endaweni yangempela.

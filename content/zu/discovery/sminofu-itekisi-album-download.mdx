## Izingoma Ezivelele Namathiphu Okulalela

Ungalalela nganoma yikuphi ukulandelana, kodwa izingoma ezimbalwa zifanele zinakwe kuqala.

- **"iTekisi" (ingoma 2, 4:06)** — ingoma eyinhloko kanye nekhadi le-albhamu lokuzethula. Qala lapha uma unemizuzu emine kuphela.
- **"Igeja" (ingoma 5, 6:05)** — ingoma ende kunazo zonke kule albhamu ngokugqama okucacile, futhi iyona enikeza i-albhamu indawo enkulu yokuphefumula.
- **"Ngithunjiwe" (ingoma 7) kanye "Ngo December" (ingoma 15)** — ukuhlanganyela okubili kukaLondeka Shangase, kuhlukaniswe phakathi nengxenye yokugcina yohlu lwezingoma.
- **"Emabhilidini" (ingoma 9)** — ukuhlanganyela kuka-Ojakalasi, futhi ingenye yezingoma ezimfushane ezigcwele ku-3:20.
- **"eMachobeni" (ingoma 17, 3:43)** — ingoma yokugcina, inegama elifanayo nokunye ukukhishwa kuka-2026.

Uhlelo olusebenzayo lokulalela: lalela i-albhamu kusukela ekuqaleni kuze kube sekugcineni kanye ngaphandle kokweqa, ukuze ukuhamba kusuka ku-intro kuze kuyofika engomeni yokugcina kube nomqondo. Emzuliswaneni wesibili, qonda ngqo ezingomeni ezihlanganyelwe. Kowesithathu, uzokwazi ukuthi yiziphi izingoma ezine noma ezinhlanu ezifanele indawo unomphela ohlwini lwengoma ojwayele ukulalela — futhi yizo okufanele uziqale ngokulanda uma isikhala sesitoreji sincane.

Elinye iphuzu mayelana nedatha: i-albhamu ethatha isikhathi esingaphezu kwehora iyingxenye ebalulekile yohlelo lweselula. Hlela ukulanda i-albhamu yakwa-Sminofu ethi iTekisi nge-Wi-Fi ekhaya, bese ugcina amafayela ukuze uwasebenzise ohambweni lwansuku zonke esikhundleni sokusakaza kabili.

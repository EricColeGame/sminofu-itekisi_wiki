## Amathiphu Asebenzayo Wokusakaza Okungcono kwe-Sminofu iTekisi

Imikhuba emincane embalwa yenza ukulalela kube lula, ikakhulukazi kudatha yeselula.

- **Landa ngaphambi kokuhamba.** Gcina i-albhamu egcwele yemizuzu engu-66 ku-Wi-Fi futhi ugweme ukulinda lapho usendleleni.
- **Qala usebenzise inkinobho yokulalela isiqeshana.** Izifinyezo ze-Apple Music zikuvumela ukuthi uzwe ithrekhi ngayinye ngaphambi kokunquma indawo yokulanda.
- **Yakha uhlu lwamathrekhi lwezingoma ezinezivakashi.** Hlanganisa amathrekhi amahlanu anezivakashi ohlwini olulodwa ukuze uthole uhambo lwemizuzu engu-17 lwezinto ezigqamile.
- **Landela ikhasi lomculi.** Ama-single amasha nokufakwa kuhlu lwamathrekhi kuvela lapho kuqala, okuyindlela abalandeli abaningi abathola ngayo izibuyekezo.
- **Hlola isilungiselelo sekhwalithi yomsindo.** Ukusakaza kwekhwalithi ephezulu kuzwakala kungcono ngokuphawulekayo emsebenzini wesiginci we-Maskandi onezendlalelo, kodwa kusebenzisa idatha eningi.
- **Faka ingoma eyisihloko eceleni kwe-"Igeja."** Ukuqhathanisa lezi ezimbili kukunika umuzwa osheshayo wobubanzi be-albhamu, kusukela ku-single emfushane kuya ekuhlelweni okunwetshiwe.

Uma ulalela kakhulu kusipika sefoni, ama-headphone azoshintsha umuzwa ngaphezu kwanoma yisiphi isilungiselelo se-equalizer — imigqa yesiginci edonswa ngeminwe echaza i-Maskandi kulula ukuyilahlekela egumbini elinomsindo.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe iTekisi kaSminofu iyatholakala ukuba isakazwe manje?**
Yebo. I-albhamu yakhishwa ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, ngaphansi kwe-EMPIRE, futhi uhlu olugcwele lwamathrekhi angu-17 lukhona ku-Apple Music nezifinyezo. Ezinye izinkundla ngokuvamile ziyalandela, kodwa ukutholakala kungahluka ngezwe, ngakho sesha ngegama lomculi uma isixhumanisi esiqondile singalayishi.

**I-albhamu inde kangakanani, futhi inezingoma ezingaki?**
iTekisi inamathrekhi angu-17 futhi ithatha ihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6, okulingana cishe nemizuzu engu-3 nemizuzwana engu-55 ingoma ngayinye. Ingxenye emfushane kakhulu isethulo semizuzu engu-1:28; ende kakhulu i-"Igeja" emizuzu engu-6:05.

**Bangobani abaculi abayizivakashi ku-iTekisi?**
Izivakashi ezine zivela kumathrekhi amahlanu: uLondeka Shangase ku-"Ngithunjiwe" naku-"Ngo December," u-Ojakalasi ku-"Emabhilidini," uNjabulo Langa ku-"Umalumekazi," noZanefa Ngidi ku-"Njengobaba."

**Iyiphi indlela engcono kakhulu yokuqala ukusakaza i-Sminofu iTekisi okokuqala?**
Dlala ingoma eyisihloko, bese kuba yi-"Igeja," bese kuba ingoma kaLondeka Shangase ethi "Ngithunjiwe." Lokho kulandelana kwezingoma ezintathu kumboza ububanzi be-albhamu cishe emizuzwini engu-14 futhi kukutshela ngokushesha ukuthi uyafuna yini ukulalela yonke into kusukela ekuqaleni kuze kube sekugcineni.

**Ingabe i-albhamu ihlanganisa ithrekhi yesethulo engingayeqa?**
Ungayeqa, kodwa i-"Benilindele Bani (Intro)" ibeka umoya ngaphansi kwemizuzwana engu-90 futhi ingena ngqo engomeni eyisihloko, ngakho abalaleli abaningi bayayigcina endaweni yayo ekudlaleni kokuqala.

## Ungayilalela kanjani i-albhamu entsha ka-Sminofu iTekisi ka-2026

Irekhodi elinamathrekhi angu-17 lifanele uhlelo. Nalu uhlelo olulula, oluphindaphindwayo olusebenza noma ngabe unemizuzu eyishumi noma ubusuku obugcwele.

1. **Qala ngengoma eyisihloko.** Dlala i-“iTekisi” (ithrekhi 2, 4:06) kuqala. Yisitatimende esicacile kakhulu senhloso futhi ikutshela phakathi nemizuzu emine ukuthi ingabe isiqondiso se-albhamu siyakufanela yini.
2. **Landela uchungechunge lomculi eyedwa.** Amathrekhi 1 kuya ku-6 cishe wonke ngu-Sminofu. Lalela lelo qoqo njengeyunithi ngaphambi kokuthinta ukusebenzisana.
3. **Gxuma ezivakashini okwesibili.** Amathrekhi 7, 9, 12, 14, no-15 ngamunye wethula izwi elisha. Ukuzwa zilandelana kudalula ukuthi izivakashi zishintsha kanjani amandla e-albhamu.
4. **Gcina amathrekhi amade esikhathini sokugxila.** I-“Igeja” (6:05) ne-“Iyeke Leyondaba” (5:11) zivuzwa ukunakwa kunokudlalwa kwangemuva.
5. **Gcina ngengoma yokugcina.** I-“eMachobeni” ifika ekugcineni ngesizathu — ixhumanisa irekhodi nokunye okukhishwe u-Sminofu ngo-2026.

## Imicabango yokugcina

I-albhamu entsha ka-Sminofu iTekisi ka-2026 iyiphrojekthi enesibindi, enomusa: izingoma eziyishumi nesikhombisa, cishe imizuzu engu-66, izivakashi ezinhlanu, nesakhiwo esivuzayo ukulalelwa kwesibili nokwesithathu. Iphinde iqinisekise isigqi sokukhishwa esizinzile esibambe kusukela ngo-2023, kanti i-iTekisi ime njengesitatimende esikhulu kakhulu salolo chungechunge.

Qala ngengoma eyisihloko, nikeza isigaba somculi eyedwa ukunakwa okugcwele, futhi vumela izivakashi zenze lokho okufanele zikwenze — zengeze umbala ngaphandle kokuthatha indawo. Yenza lokho, futhi ihora lizodlula ngokushesha kunalokho okushiwo isikhathi sokudlala.

## Imibuzo Evamile

**Usuku lokukhishwa kwe-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi luyini?**
Imibiko yomphakathi ichaza i-*iTekisi* njengekhona, kodwa ukufakwa kuhlu okukodwa okunosuku, okusemthethweni akukashicilelwa ngokungaguquki kuzo zonke izinkundla. Indlela esheshayo yokuqinisekisa **usuku lokukhishwa kwe-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi** esifundeni sakho ukuvula ikhasi le-albhamu kusevisi enkulu yokusakaza futhi ufunde usuku nomugqa we-copyright ngqo.

**Ingabe i-*iTekisi* isiphumile manje?**
Imibhalo yabalandeli neyomphakathi iyimemezele njengephumile, futhi leyo mibhalo iyisizathu esihle sokuhlola. Ukutholakala kungahluka ngezwe nangenkundla, ngakho qinisekisa esitolo sakho ngaphambi kokucabanga ukuthi iyasebenza lapho ukhona.

**Ngingaziswa kanjani ngokushesha lapho ukukhishwa kuqinisekisiwe?**
Landela ama-akhawunti aqinisekisiwe ka-Sminofu, londoloza i-albhamu kusengaphambili uma isixhumanisi sikhona, futhi londoloza iphrofayili yomculi kuhlelo lwakho lokusakaza. Abaculi abalondoloziwe ngokuvamile bavusa isaziso lapho kukhishwa okusha kuvela.

**Ingabe usuku lokukhishwa luyahluka ngezwe?**
Kungase kwenzeke. Ukukhishwa kwaphakathi kwamabili kusakazeka ngesikhathi sendawo, futhi abanye abasabalalisi bahambisa ezimakethe ezithile kuqala. Uma umngane wakwelinye izwe ekwazi ukudlala i-*iTekisi* kanti wena awukwazi, lokho kuvamile — yinike amahora ambalwa bese uvuselela ikhasi le-albhamu.

Umugqa ophansi: **usuku lokukhishwa kwe-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi** luqinisekiswa kangcono ngendlela eyisicefe — amakhasi ekhathalogi, imigqa ye-copyright, neziteshini zomculi ngokwakhe — hhayi ngendlela ejabulisayo. Yenza lokho, futhi uzohlala uyena umuntu osengxoxweni yeqembu onempendulo efanele.

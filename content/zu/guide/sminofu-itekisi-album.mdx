Amathiphu ambalwa asebenzayo ngaphezu kwalolo kulandelana:

- **Yakha uhlu lwadlalwayo lwezivakashi kuphela.** Amathrekhi amahlanu ayisingeniso esifushane nesicacile ohlangothini lwe-albhamu lokusebenzisana futhi kuthatha ngaphansi kwemizuzu eyishumi nesishiyagalombili ukuwaqeda.
- **Sebenzisa ithebula lobude ukuhlela.** Uma unohambo lwemizuzu engu-40, amathrekhi 1 kuya ku-10 athatha cishe imizuzu engu-39 — indawo yokuma engokwemvelo ngqo ngaphambi kuka-“Es'thenjini.”
- **Lalela ingoma enesihloko se-albhamu kabili.** Ku-4:06 ayiyona inde kunawo wonke noma emfishane kunawo wonke lapha, kodwa ithwala igama le-albhamu ngesizathu, futhi iyakwazi ukuphindwa.
- **Hlola ikhasi le-albhamu elisemthethweni** ukuqinisekisa ukulandelana kwamanje kwamathrekhi nokutholakala esifundeni sakho — ungakwazi [ukusakaza iTekisi ku-Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/0WcvW7hJWCFvf4lP6uKO9E) futhi ubone imininingwane egcwele yabahlanganyeli ngqo.

Njengakuwo wonke ama-albhamu, imibiko yomphakathi yokuthi yimaphi amathrekhi athinta kakhulu izohluka kumlaleli nomlaleli. Abanye abantu bakhangwa izivakashi, abanye amathrekhi amade eyedwa, futhi impikiswano ye-shuffle nokulandelana ayipheli ngempela. Iseluleko esisodwa esingaguquki ukunikeza i-albhamu ukulalela okuphelele ngaphambi kokuthatha isinqumo.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi?**
Kunamathrekhi angu-17 esewonke, athatha ihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6. Emfishane kunazo zonke isethulo semizuzwana engu-1:28, u-“Benilindele Bani,” futhi ende kunazo zonke ngu-“Igeja” ku-6:05.

**Ubani ovela ku-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi?**
Abaculi abayizivakashi abane bavela kumathrekhi amahlanu: u-Londeka Shangase ku-“Ngithunjiwe” naku-“Ngo December,” u-Ojakalasi ku-“Emabhilidini,” u-Njabulo Langa ku-“Umalumekazi,” no-Zanefa Ngidi ku-“Njengobaba.” Izingoma ezisele eziyishumi nambili zika-Sminofu eyedwa.

**Yakhishwa nini i-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi?**
Yakhishwa ngo-Okthoba 16, 2026, futhi u-Sminofu / EMPIRE ubalwa njengomnikazi wamalungelo okukopisha nawokurekhoda walowo nyaka.

**Iyiphi indlela engcono yokuyilalela okokuqala?**
Yidlale ngokulandelana, uqale ngesethulo futhi uqede ku-“eMachobeni,” futhi gwema i-shuffle uze uyizwe yonke kanye. Izingoma ezihlanganyelwe ziqoqene phakathi kwamathrekhi 7 no-15, ngakho ukulalela ngokulandelana kuyindlela kuphela yokuzwa ukuthi zihlukaniswe kanjani. Ngemuva kwalokho, umzuliswano we-shuffle uyisivivinyo esihle sokuthi imaphi amathrekhi azimela yedwa.

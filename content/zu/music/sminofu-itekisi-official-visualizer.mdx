I-visualizer ivame ukuba yindlela esheshayo yokuzwa ingoma iphelele ngenkathi ubona ubuciko, amagama engoma, noma umoya umculi ayewuhlosile, yingakho abalaleli abaningi bafuna i-visualizer esemthethweni ye-Sminofu iTekisi ngaphambi kokuzibophezela ekulaleleni okugcwele.

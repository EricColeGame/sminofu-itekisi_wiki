### Izikhathi Okufanele Uziqale Ngazo

Uma ufuna indlela emfushane ngaphambi kokuzibophezela kuyo yonke into, lawa amaphuzu ayisakhiwo:

- **"iTekisi" (ithrekhi 2)** — ingoma esihloko ifika kusenesikhathi futhi ibeka isimo kukho konke okulandela.
- **"Igeja" (ithrekhi 5)** — ku-6:05, iyingoma ende kunazo zonke kule albhamu futhi yiyona okungenzeka kakhulu ihlukanise abalaleli.
- **"Ngithunjiwe" (ithrekhi 7)** — ukubonakala kokuqala komculi oyisivakashi, futhi yilapho ingxenye yesibili ye-albhamu iqala khona.
- **"Iyeke Leyondaba" (ithrekhi 16)** — ithrekhi engaphambi kokugcina ethatha imizuzu engu-5:11 futhi ethwala isisindo esikhulu ngaphambi kwengoma yokugcina.
- **"eMachobeni" (ithrekhi 17)** — ingoma yokugcina inegama elifanayo nokunye ukukhishwa kuka-Sminofu kuka-2026, okukwenza kube yindawo ethakazelisayo yokuqhathanisa.

Qaphela ukuthi amathrekhi amabili amade kunazo zonke — "Igeja" no-"Iyeke Leyondaba" — akhona eduze kwaphakathi naseduze kokugcina. Lokho kuwukukhetha ngamabomu kwesigqi: i-albhamu ikunika isikhala sokuphumula ekuqaleni, bese icela ukunaka kwakho kamuva.

## Ungayilalela Kanjani I-Albhamu Ephelele ye-Sminofu iTekisi

Imizuzu engamashumi ayisithupha nesithupha iyisibopho sangempela emnothweni wamapulayilisti. Le ndlela yemijikelo emithathu yenza i-albhamu ilula ukumunca ngaphandle kokuyenza ibe umsindo wangemuva.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Ukusuka phambili kuya emuva | Dlala i-albhamu ngokulandelana, ungashintshi, ungatshimi | Wonke angu-17 | ~imizuzu engu-66 |
| 2. Isihloko nezindawo eziyisisekelo | Phinda udlale izikhathi eziyisakhiwo | 2, 5, 7, 16, 17 | ~imizuzu engu-24 |
| 3. Umjikelo wezivakashi | Lalela kuphela amathrekhi okusebenzisana | 7, 9, 12, 14, 15 | ~imizuzu engu-18 |

Amathiphu ambalwa asebenzayo:

- **Cisha ukushintshwa kwezingoma.** Ithrekhi yokuqala ingu-1:28 futhi ikhona ukuze ilungiselele i-"iTekisi" — ukuyilalela ingekho ohlelweni lwayo kuyamosha.
- **Sebenzisa umjikelo wesibili kumazwi engoma.** Izihloko zesiZulu ziklomelisa ukufunda okuseduze uma usuzazi ukuthi imiculo ihamba kanjani.
- **Gcina i-"Igeja" isikhathi lapho ungakwazi ukunaka khona.** Njengoba idlula imizuzu eyisithupha, iyithrekhi okungenzeka kakhulu ukuthi idlulwe uma ungenakho ukunaka.
- **Qhathanisa i-"eMachobeni" nokukhishwa okuzimele kuka-2026.** Iyindlela eshesha kakhulu yokuzwa ukuthi ingoma eyodwa ishintsha kanjani ibe yingoma yokugcina ye-albhamu.

Uma unemizuzu engamashumi amabili kuphela, umjikelo wesibili yiwona okufanele uwukhethe. Ukunika ithrekhi esihloko, ingoma ende kunazo zonke, isivakashi sokuqala, kanye nombhangqwana wokugcina — ngamafuphi uhlaka lwe-albhamu.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-albhamu ephelele ye-Sminofu iTekisi?**
Amathrekhi ayishumi nesikhombisa, isikhathi sisonke siyihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6. I-intro engu-1:28 kuphela engaphansi kwemizuzu emithathu.

**Ngobani abaculi abayizivakashi ku-iTekisi?**
Abaculi abane kumathrekhi amahlanu: uLondeka Shangase (kabili, ku-"Ngithunjiwe" naku-"Ngo December"), u-Ojakalasi ku-"Emabhilidini," uNjabulo Langa ku-"Umalumekazi," noZanefa Ngidi ku-"Njengobaba."

**Ngingayilalela kuphi i-albhamu ephelele ye-Sminofu iTekisi?**

**Ingabe iTekisi iyindawo enhle yokuqala kubalaleli abaqalayo?**
Yebo. Amathrekhi ayo angu-17 ahlanganisa izinhlobonhlobo eziningi ukudlula noma yikuphi ukukhishwa kwangaphambili kuka-Sminofu, ngakho isebenza njengokulalela kokuqala nokujula — nakuba abalandeli bama-albhamu ka-2023–2025 bazobona izinto eziningi ezijwayelekile.

**Iyiphi ithrekhi ende kunazo zonke ku-albhamu?**
"Igeja" ku-6:05, ilandelwa yi-"Iyeke Leyondaba" ku-5:11. Konke okunye kungena phakathi kuka-1:28 no-4:22.

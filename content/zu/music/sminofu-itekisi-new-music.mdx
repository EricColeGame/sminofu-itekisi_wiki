Irekhodi lihlelwe njengeqoqo lomculo wangqo: isingeniso esifushane esifana nokukhuluma, bese kuba uchungechunge lwezingoma ezigcwele ezishintshana phakathi kokucabanga nesigqi.

Ingoma eyisihloko ihlezi endaweni yesibili ohlwini, okuyinto engajwayelekile ku-albhamu eyabelana ngegama nengoma. Ukwazi okushiwo isihloko kushintsha indlela okufundwa ngayo ivesi, ngisho noma ungakhulumi isiZulu ngokushelelayo.

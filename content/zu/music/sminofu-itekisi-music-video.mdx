## Iyini Ngempela "iTekisi"

*iTekisi* iyingoma eyinhloko nengoma yesibili ku-albhamu ka-Sminofu ka-2026 enegama elifanayo, ethatha imizuzu engu-4:06. Igama elithi *itekisi* igama lesiZulu elisho itekisi — igama elithwala umhlaba wonke wencazelo ekuphileni kwansuku zonke eNingizimu Afrika. Amarende amatekisi, imizila emide, nabalingiswa abahamba kuyo yizindawo ezijwayelekile zokuxoxa indaba ku-Maskandi, uhlobo lwesiZulu oluhambisana nesiginci olwakhiwe phezu kokuxoxa indaba, ukuhlabelela okuphendulana, nokuqapha okubukhali komphakathi.

Leso sihloko ngesinye sezizathu esenza isidingo se-**vidiyo yomculo ye-Sminofu iTekisi** sibe phezulu kangaka. Ingoma ekhuluma ngamasiko amatekisi icela ngokucacile ukwenziwa kwezithombe — amarende, iminyango evulekayo, abagibeli abanezindaba zabo. ## Ingabe Sekukhona Ividiyo Yomculo Esemthethweni ye-Sminofu iTekisi?

Nayi impendulo eqotho ngokusho kwalokhu okubhaliwe: ayikho **ividiyo yomculo esemthethweni ye-Sminofu iTekisi** eqinisekisiwe etholakele emithonjeni ehlobene nokukhishwa kwe-albhamu. Okubhalwe mayelana ne-*iTekisi* kubonisa i-albhamu ephelele yokulalela ku-inthanethi — uhlu lwezingoma, izikhathi, ababambiqhaza, uphawu — hhayi ividiyo eshicilelwe. Lokho akusho ukuthi izithombe ngeke zivele; kusho ukuthi abalandeli kufanele baqaphele okulayishiwe okungaqinisekisiwe kuze kube yilapho isiteshi esisemthethweni sishicilela okukodwa.

Okujwayele ukwenzeka ngengoma eyinhloko ye-Maskandi kucacile. Ingoma iqala ngokukhishwa ukuze ilalelwe ku-inthanethi, izinhlu zokudlala ziyithatha, bese izithombe zilandelwa lapho i-albhamu isithole abalaleli bayo. *iTekisi* isivele yafakwa ezinhleni zokudlala ezihleliwe ezihlanganisa i-New Music Daily ne-Maskandi Hits, okuvame ukuba yisigaba lapho isabelomali sezithombe siba nokwenzeka.

**Imibiko yomphakathi** evela kubalandeli abalandela ukukhishwa ibonisa ukuthi umsindo ubesakazwa kabanzi kusukela maphakathi noMfumfu, abalaleli babelana ngamavesi abawathandayo kunamakliphu. Kuze kube kuvele okulayishiwe okusemthethweni, umcabango ophephile ukuthi noma iyiphi ividiyo "esemthethweni" ohlangana nayo iwukulayishwa kabusha okusebenzisa isithombe se-albhamu njengesithombe esimile.

## Kungani Izithombe Zibalulekile Ku-Maskandi

I-Maskandi iwuhlobo lapho ukudlala kuyingxenye yesigamu se-albhamu. Ku-albhamu ekhishwe maphakathi noMfumfu, izithombe futhi ziyindlela i-albhamu ehlala iphila ngayo ezinyangeni ezilandelayo kunokuba iphele ngemuva kweviki lokukhishwa.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe ikhona ividiyo yomculo esemthethweni ye-Sminofu iTekisi njengamanje?**
Ayikho ividiyo esemthethweni eqinisekisiwe etholakele ezintweni zokukhishwa ezihlobene ne-albhamu. Okukhishiwe okubhaliwe i-albhamu yokulalela ku-inthanethi enezingoma ezingu-17, ngakho noma iyiphi ividiyo ethi isemthethweni kufanele ihlolwe eziteshini eziqinisekisiwe zika-Sminofu ne-EMPIRE ngaphambi kokuba uyithembe.

**Ingoma ethi "iTekisi" ithatha isikhathi esingakanani?**
Ingoma eyinhloko ithatha u-4:06 futhi ihlezi kungoma yesibili ku-albhamu, ngemuva nje kwesingeniso esingu-1:28 esithi "Benilindele Bani."

**Ngingayilalela kuphi i-albhamu ngokusemthethweni ku-inthanethi?**
I-albhamu ephelele iyatholakala ku-Apple Music naku-iTunes Store, ibhalwe ngaphansi kuka-Sminofu nekhredithi yophawu i-EMPIRE. Ukulalela lapho kuyindlela ethembeke kakhulu yokuzwa inguqulo eyinhloko esemthethweni.

**Kufanele ngenzeni uma ngithola ividiyo yomculo ye-Sminofu iTekisi ebukeka ingamanga?**
Hlola isiteshi ukuthi sinabheji yokuqinisekisa yini, qinisekisa ukuthi incazelo ikhredithi uSminofu noma i-EMPIRE, futhi qhathanisa isikhathi nesingu-4:06. Uma okunye kwalokho kuhluleka, bika okulayishiwe futhi uqhubeke ubuka iziteshi ezisemthethweni — yilapho ukukhishwa kwangempela kuzovela khona kuqala.

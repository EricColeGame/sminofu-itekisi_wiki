I-albhamu enemikhondo engu-17 yafika ngo-16 Okthoba 2026, iletha umculo omusha ongaphezu kwehora ovela kumculi obesakha ikhathalogi yakhe ngokuqhubekayo kusukela ngo-2023. Uma usuzwe izingoma ezodwa futhi uzibuza ukuthi ingabe iphrojekthi ephelele ihlangana kahle, impendulo ingu-yebo ecacile — kodwa imininingwane iyona eyenza kube nesizathu sokuchitha isikhathi sayo. Ukwazi ukuthi i-**Sminofu Itekisi** iqukethe ini, ubani oyisivakashi kuyo, nokuthi ihambisana kanjani namarekhodi angaphambili kwenza ukulalela kokuqala okuhle kube okungcono kakhulu.

Le albhamu ivala unyaka okhiqize kakhulu. USminofu wakhipha i-“eMachobeni” ne-“Woza Friday (Remake)” ngo-2026 ngaphambi kokuhlanganisa le phrojekthi yobude obugcwele, futhi ithrekhi yokugcina ye-albhamu inegama elifanayo naleyo ngoma eyodwa yangaphambili — ukuhlangana okuhle okubonisa ukulandelana okucatshangelwe ngamabomu kunokuqoqwa okungahleliwe kwezingoma.

Okugqama kakhulu ubukhulu bayo. Ngamathrekhi angu-17 cishe nemizuzu engu-66 yomculo, i-Sminofu Itekisi inikeza abalaleli isikhathi sokungena kahle. Kunendawo ezinhlanu kuphela zezivakashi kulo lonke irekhodi, okusho ukuthi ingxenye enkulu ye-albhamu incike emculweni womculi uqobo. Kubalaleli abafuna ukuzwa umculi uqobo kunabalingisi abashintshayo, lokho kuyiphuzu elivuna le albhamu.

- **Ungahluleli i-albhamu ngethrekhi yesibili kuphela.** Ithrekhi eyisihloko inamandla, kodwa ububanzi berekhodi bubonakala esigabeni esiphakathi, phakathi kwamathrekhi esihlanu neshumi nambili.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**I-Sminofu Itekisi yakhishwa nini?**

I-albhamu yakhishwa ngo-16 Okthoba 2026, ngaphansi kwe-Sminofu / EMPIRE. Yandulelwa ngawo lowo nyaka yi-“eMachobeni” ne-“Woza Friday (Remake),” futhi ithrekhi yayo yokugcina inesihloko esifanayo nesawo wokuqala.

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-Sminofu Itekisi?**

Kunamathrekhi angu-17 anesikhathi esiphelele esicishe sibe ihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6. Amahlanu kuphela alawo mathrekhi ahlanganisa umculi oyisivakashi, futhi isingeniso yisona esifushane kunazo zonke ku-1:28.

**Ubani uLondeka Shangase kule albhamu?**

ULondeka Shangase ungumhlanganyeli ovele kakhulu ku-Sminofu Itekisi, evela ku-“Ngithunjiwe” (ithrekhi 7) naku-“Ngo December” (ithrekhi 15). Zombili izingoma zisendaweni ezinengqondo — enye ingemuva nje kwephuzu eliphakathi, enye ikota yokuvala — okwenza kube lula ukuba zibe yizindawo zokungena kubalaleli abasha.

**Ngingayilalela kuphi i-albhamu ephelele?**

Irekhodi eliphelele liyatholakala ezinkundleni ezinkulu zokusakaza, kuhlanganise ne-Spotify, lapho uhlu lwamathrekhi, ubude bawo, namakhredithi kukhonjiswa ngokugcwele. Uma ufuna ukuqhathanisa i-albhamu nokukhishwe ngaphambilini, ikhasi lephrofayela lomculi luhluza yonke eminye imisebenzi endaweni eyodwa.

**Kufanelekile yini ukuyilalela ngokulandelana?**

Yebo. Ngamathrekhi angu-17 nesakhiwo esicatshangelwe ngamabomu — isingeniso esifushane, ithrekhi eyisihloko ekuqaleni, ingoma ende emaphakathi kuthrekhi yesihlanu, nesigaba sokuvala esinamandla — indlela i-albhamu ehamba ngayo iyingxenye yokuzizwa. Ukuxuba kuyayicindezela lokho, ikakhulukazi ekulaleleni kokuqala.

## Kungani Ukusesha kwe-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi Kubalulekile

Ezinye izingoma ze-albhamu zenzelwe inkinobho yokweqa. Ezinye ziba isizathu sokuthi abantu bacindezele ukudlala i-albhamu yonke. Kubalaleli abaningi, ukuxhumana kwe-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi kuseqenjini lesibili — ukubonakala kwesivakashi esisodwa okufihlwe engomeni yesi-14 okuguqula i-albhamu ende ibe ingxoxo.

Uma useshe leyo binzana lamagama amathathu, cishe wezwa kuqala ethi "Njengobaba" wabe usufuna umongo. Yileyo ndlela ukuhlangana kwe-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi kusebenza ngayo: ikhredithi imfushane, umbono awumfushane. Lo mhlahlandlela uchaza lapho ingoma ihlezi khona ku-albhamu, ukuthi amakhredithi ashoni ngempela, ukuthi isivakashi sihambisana kanjani nesakhiwo serekhodi, nokuthi ungalalela kanjani ngendlebe engcono.

Inguqulo emfushane: *iTekisi* iyiphrojekthi enezingoma ezingu-17, futhi ukubonakala kukaZanefa Ngidi kungenye yezikhala ezine kuphela zezivakashi kuyo. Ukuqonda lokho kuhlelwa kuchaza okuningi ngendlela i-albhamu eyakhiwe ngayo.

## Ubani uZanefa Ngidi, futhi Kungani i-"Njengobaba" Isebenza Kahle

Nakhu lapho ukusesha okuningi kukhungatheka khona. Irekhodi lomphakathi ngoZanefa Ngidi lincane — ubalwa njengomculi wesivakashi ku-"Njengobaba," futhi lelo khredithi liyindawo ethembeke kakhulu etholakalayo. Noma yini engaphezu kwalokho (i-discography ephelele, umlando welebula, isigaba sohlobo) ayibhalwanga kahle emithonjeni efinyelelekayo, futhi ukuphinda umlando ongaqinisekisiwe kungalimaza abafundi.

Okungashiwo ngokuqiniseka yilokho okuboniswa yirekhodi ngokwalo:

- **Ukubonakala kwesivakashi ukusebenzisana kwangempela kwesitayela se-duet, hhayi isampula noma ithegi yangemuva.** Le ngoma ifakwe njengo-"Sminofu, Zanefa Ngidi," okuyindlela yekhredithi esetshenziswa yi-Spotify ekusebenzeni ngokuhlanganyela kwangempela.
- **Ithatha yonke imizuzu engu-4:12.** Ukubonakala kwesivakashi okuthatha ubude obuphelele bengoma kuziphatha ngendlela ehlukile kunokubonakala kwemizuzwana engu-20 — kushintsha isakhiwo sengoma, hhayi nje indlela eyakhiwe ngayo.
- **Ifika sekwephuzile ngokulandelana.** Izivakashi zasekupheleni kwe-albhamu zivame ukuthwala isisindo semizwa hhayi isisindo sezentengiselwano. Zibekwa ukuze zixazulule okuthile, hhayi ukudayisa iculo lokuqala.
- **Isihloko ngokwaso sikhombisa ubudlelwano.** Ethi "Njengobaba" ihumusha cishe ngokuthi "njengobaba wami" ngesiZulu, okubeka le ngoma endaweni yozalo, ifa, nokufana komndeni — indawo engokwemvelo yamazwi amabili ashintshanayo emigqeni.

Lelo phuzu lokugcina yingakho ukuhlangana kwe-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi kufundwa njengokungaphezu kwekhredithi. Ingoma ebekwe eduze komuntu ongubaba imema indlela yokuphendulana, futhi isakhiwo se-duet sinikeza ingqikithi imibono emibili esikhundleni soyedwa. Kungakhathaliseki ukuthi ingoma ingeyomlando womuntu noma iyindaba, ukukhethwa kokuhlelwa kuhambisana nesihloko.

**3. Sebenzisa imephu yesikhathi ukuhlela izikhathi zokulalela.** I-albhamu ihlukana ngokucacile engomeni yesi-9 — cishe emizuzwini engu-34. Leyo yindawo yemvelo yokuma uma ulalela usohambeni.

### Isipiliyoni Somdlali Nemibiko Yomphakathi

Phakathi kwabalaleli abachithe isikhathi nerekhodi, kuvela izindikimba ezimbalwa kaningi. Lokhu kuyisipiliyoni somdlali nemibiko yomphakathi hhayi idatha yamashadi eqinisekisiwe, ngakho kuthatha njengemibono hhayi amaqiniso:

- Ingxenye yokugcina ye-albhamu ivame ukuchazwa njengenamandla kunengxenye yokuqala, kanti izivakashi zibalulwa njengesizathu.
- "Igeja," engu-6:05 ingoma ende kunazo zonke, iphawulwa njengengoma ekhulayo — kulula ukuyiqa ekuqaleni, kunzima ukuyeka ukuyiphindaphinda kamuva.
- "Njengobaba" ivame ukubizwa ngokuthi isiqongo semizwa, futhi ukuhlangana kwe-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi kuvame ukuba yindawo yokungena yabalandeli abasha abafike ngamakiliphu hhayi nge-albhamu.
- Abanye abalaleli baphawula ukuthi i-intro isebenza kangcono ngokulandelana kunokuba ingoma ezimele, okuyinto ejwayelekile ezingomeni zokuqala.

## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa

**Ingabe "Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi" ibhekisela kunani ngempela?**
Ikhombisa ikhredithi ethile ku-albhamu kaSminofu ka-2026 ethi *iTekisi*: ingoma yesi-14, ethi "Njengobaba," edlalwa uSminofu nomculi wesivakashi uZanefa Ngidi. La magama amathathu awazona izingxenye ezihlukene — achaza ingoma eyodwa ku-albhamu eyodwa.

**Iphi ingoma efaka uZanefa Ngidi ku-iTekisi?**
Ingoma yesi-14, ethi "Njengobaba," ethatha imizuzu engu-4 namasekhondi angu-12. Ingenye yesine futhi ende kunazo zonke ezivakashini ezine ze-albhamu, futhi ihlezi ngaphansi kwemizuzu engu-50 ngaphakathi kwerekhodi.

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-iTekisi, futhi ide kangakanani?**
Izingoma eziyishumi nesikhombisa ezingamahora angu-1 nemizuzu engu-6 isiyonke, ngobude obujwayelekile bengoma obucishe bube imizuzu engu-3 namasekhondi angu-55. Emfushane i-intro engu-1:28; ende kunazo zonke i-"Igeja" engu-6:05.

**Ingabe i-"Njengobaba" ikhishwe njengeculo elizimele?**
Uhlu olutholakalayo luyibonisa njengengoma ye-albhamu ku-*iTekisi* hhayi njengeculo elikhuthazwe ngokwehlukile. Ngokuphawulekayo, i-"eMachobeni" — ingoma yokuvala ye-albhamu — ivela njengokukhishwa kwayo emlandweni wezingoma zikaSminofu, okusikisela ukuthi isu lamaculo laligxile kwenye indawo.

## Isiphetho

Isikhathi se-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi siyikhredithi encane enendima enkulu ngokwedlulele. Sihlezi sijulile ku-albhamu enezingoma ezingu-17, sithatha isikhathi eside kunanoma yikuphi okunye ukuhlanganyela kwerekhodi, futhi isihloko saso sisibeka endaweni yomndeni nefa — uhlobo lwengqikithi olunikeza umvuzo ekuhlelweni kwamazwi amabili.

Uma ufike ngenxa yesivakashi, hlala ngenxa yokulandelana. Qala engomeni yesi-14, bese uhlehlela emuva ku-"Imidlwembe" futhi uqhubekele phambili ku-"Ngo December." Uzozwa ukuthi kungani indawo eyodwa yesivakashi ku-albhamu yemizuzu engu-66 yaba yinto abantu abayifuna ngegama.

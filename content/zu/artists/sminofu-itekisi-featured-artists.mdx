## Kungani Abaculi Abahlanganyeli ku-Sminofu Itekisi Babalulekile

Qala ngezinombolo, ngoba zichaza isu. I-*iTekisi* yafika ngo-16 Okthoba 2026, nge-Sminofu / EMPIRE, futhi inamathrekhi angu-17 cishe esikhathini esiyihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6. Amahlanu kuphela alawo mathrekhi anekhredithi yesivakashi — cishe u-29% we-albhamu ngokubalwa kwamathrekhi. Uma kulinganiswa ngesikhathi sokudlala, izingoma ezinhlanu ezihlanganyelwe zihlangana zibe cishe imizuzu engu-17 nemizuzwana engu-49, noma cishe u-27% wesikhathi sokuphindwa.

Ukuzibamba kuyona iyona nhloso. I-albhamu enamathrekhi angu-17 inika umculi ithuba elikhulu lokwabela abahlanganyeli; uSminofu usebenzisa abahlanganyeli abane futhi unika oyedwa kuphela ithuba lesibili. Umphumela uyicwecwe lapho ukuvela komvakashi kusazwakala njengesigameko esikhulu hhayi into yokugcwalisa.

## Umvakashi Ngamunye Uletha Ini — Nokuthi Uzwa Kanjani

Abaculi abahlanganyeli ku-Sminofu Itekisi abashintsheki. Isikhala ngasinye sinesimo esihlukile, futhi izikhathi zokudlala zodwa zisikisela ukuthi zakhiwa kanjani.

### Londeka Shangase: Umhlanganyeli Wamahlandla Amabili

UShangase uthola ithuba elikhulu kunabo bonke ku-albhamu, futhi womabili amathrekhi akhe amfushane. "Ngithunjiwe" ithatha u-3:26, "Ngo December" ithatha u-3:34 — akukho okudlula imizuzu emine. Lokho kufushane kusikisela ukuhlelwa okugxile ku-hook okwakhiwe ngokusebenzisana hhayi ngokudlala yedwa isikhathi eside. Lalela la mathrekhi amabili elandelana futhi uzwa ukuthi ukuvela kwesibili kuhambisana nokokuqala ngaphandle kokukuphinda.

### Ojakalasi: Inhlansi Phakathi Ne-Albhamu

"Emabhilidini" ithatha u-3:20, enye yezingoma ezimfushane kakhulu kule rekodi. Ibekwe ethrekhi 9, ifika ngemuva nje kwesigaba eside kakhulu sezinto zomculi oyedwa futhi ifaka ushintsho lwamandla khona lapho i-albhamu enamathrekhi angu-17 iqala ukudinga khona.

### Njabulo Langa: Ukuhlanganyela Okufushane Kakhulu

"Umalumekazi" iyona emfushane kunawo wonke amathrekhi amahlanu anabahlanganyeli, ingu-3:17. Lokho kuyenza ibe ukusebenzisana okubukhali nokonga kakhulu ku-albhamu — ukudluliswa okusheshayo hhayi iculo eliphelele lomabili. Uma ufuna ukuqonda ukuthi umhlanganyeli udinga isikhala esincane kangakanani ukuze ashiye uphawu, qala lapha.

### Zanefa Ngidi: Isisekelo Sekupheleni Kwe-Albhamu

"Njengobaba" iyona ithrekhi ende kunazo zonke ezinabahlanganyeli, ingu-4:12, futhi ihleli ethrekhi 14, ijule ngokwanele kurekhodi ukuze ithwale isisindo. Yiyona ndawo yomvakashi enesikhala sokuphefumula, futhi ilungiselela uhambo lokugcina lomculi oyedwa lwe-"Ngo December," "Iyeke Leyondaba," ne-"eMachobeni."

### Amathiphu Angokoqobo Okulalela

- **Qala ethrekhi 7.** Uma unemizuzu eyishumi kuphela, dlala "Ngithunjiwe," "Emabhilidini," ne-"Umalumekazi" ngokulandelana ukuze uzwe izindlela ezintathu ezihlukile zokuhlanganyela.
- **Qhathanisa amathrekhi amabili kaShangase.** "Ngithunjiwe" no-"Ngo December" ahlukene ngamathrekhi ayisishiyagalombili kodwa abelana umhlanganyeli; umehluko ukhombisa ukuthi ukuhlelwa kushintsha kanjani iculo lomabili.
- **Bheka izikhathi zokudlala.** Wonke amathrekhi anabahlanganyeli angaphansi kuka-4:15, kanti iculo elide kakhulu lomculi oyedwa ku-albhamu, "Igeja," lithatha u-6:05. Izivakashi zithola ukugxila, hhayi ukunwebeka.
- **Funda amakhredithi ngenkathi usakaza.** Izinkundla zokusakaza zibala abahlanganyeli ngqo emgqeni wethrekhi, okuyindlela esheshayo yokuqinisekisa ukuthi ubani kuyiphi ingoma.

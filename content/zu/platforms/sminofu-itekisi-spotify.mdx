## Ungasakaza Kanjani i-Sminofu iTekisi ku-Spotify

Ukuqalisa i-albhamu kuthatha ngaphansi komzuzu, kodwa izilungiselelo ezimbalwa ezincane zenza umehluko obonakalayo endleleni ezwakala ngayo nasekutheni uphinde uyibuyele kangaki.

1. **Vula ikhasi le-albhamu.** Sesha "Sminofu iTekisi" kubha yokusesha ye-Spotify, noma landela isixhumanisi esiqondile se-albhamu, bese ucindezela u-play kuthrekhi yokuqala esikhundleni se-shuffle.
2. **Gcina i-albhamu emtatsheni wakho.** Ukuthinta isithonjana senhliziyo noma sokwengeza kugcina i-albhamu eqoqweni lakho ukuze ivele kufidi yakho yasekhaya nasezifinyelelweni zokuphela konyaka.
3. **Landa ukuze ulalele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.** Ababhalisele i-Spotify Premium bangagcina wonke amathrekhi angu-17 endaweni. [Umhlahlandlela osemthethweni we-Spotify wokulalela ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/listen-offline/) uchaza ukuthi ukulandwa kusebenza kanjani kumadivayisi ahlukene.
4. **Landela umculi.** Ukulandela u-Sminofu kusho ukuthi ukukhishwa okusha, kuhlanganise noma yimaphi ama-single esikhathi esizayo, kufika ku-Release Radar yakho ngokuzenzakalela.
5. **Hlola isilungiselelo sekhwalithi yomsindo.** Kuselula, ikhwalithi yokusakaza ivame ukusetelwa ku-"Automatic." Ukushintshela ku-"Very High" ku-Wi-Fi kunikeza izingxube eziminyene isikhala esengeziwe sokuphefumula.
6. **Vula ukudlala okungenazikhala.** Kugcina i-intro ihamba ngqo iye kuthrekhi eyisihloko esikhundleni sokufaka ukuthula.

| Isilungiselelo | Lapho Usithola Khona | Kungani Kusiza |
|---|---|---|
| Ikhwalithi yomsindo | Izilungiselelo → Ikhwalithi Yomsindo | Igcina imininingwane ezingxubeni ezinamazinga amaningi |
| Ukulanda | Ikhasi le-albhamu → Ukushintsha kokulanda | Ukudlala ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngaphandle kokusebenzisa idatha |
| Ukudlala okungenazikhala | Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala | Ukushintsha okushelelayo phakathi kwamathrekhi |
| I-Crossfade | Izilungiselelo → Ukudlala | Kungcono ukushiye kuvaliwe kuma-albhamu ahlelwe ngokulandelana |
| Imibhalo yengoma | Okudlayo Manje → Imibhalo yengoma | Kuwusizo ekulandeleni amagama esiZulu |

Isixwayiso esisodwa: i-crossfade inhle kakhulu kumapulayilisti kodwa iyaphikisana ne-albhamu ene-intro ehlosiwe. Uma ufuna isipiliyoni se-Sminofu iTekisi Spotify esihloswe, vala i-crossfade ekulaleleni kokuqala okuphelele.

## Amathiphu Okulalela Ukuze Uthole Okwengeziwe Ku-Albhamu

Imikhuba embalwa yenza i-albhamu ende itholakale kalula, ikakhulukazi leyo eculwa ngolimi ongase ungakhulumi kahle.

- **Yilalele kabili.** Ukudlula kokuqala kwenzelwe imibono; okwesibili yilapho ukulandelana kuqondakala khona. Iningi lomvuzo kurekhodi elinamathrekhi angu-17 lufika lapho ulilalela kaningi.
- **Sebenzisa umbono wemibhalo yengoma.** Ukufunda kanye nemigqa yesiZulu ngesikhathi ingoma idlala iyindlela esheshayo yokubona amagama owawungeke uwabone.
- **Yihlukanise izingxenye ezimbili.** Amathrekhi okuqala kuya kwesishiyagalolunye athatha cishe imizuzu engu-33, okuyindawo yokuma engokwemvelo ngaphambi kokuthi ingxenye yesibili ivule nge-"Es'thenjini."
- **Yakha uhlu lokungenela.** Khipha izintandokazi ezine noma ezinhlanu uziyise kuhlu olufushane lokudlala, bese ubuyela ku-albhamu ephelele uma leyo zingoma sezizwakala zijwayelekile.
- **Lalela ngama-headphone amahle okungenani kanye.** Amazwi ahlanganisiwe namakhonsathi ahlanganisiwe avame ukufiphala kuzipikha zocingo.
- **Buyela emuva ngemva kwesonto.** I-Spotify ngezikhathi ezithile ibuyekeza amakhasi e-albhamu ngemidwebo emisha ye-canvas, imininingwane, noma amanothi angaphakathi ngemva kokuthi ukukhishwa kuzinze.

Imibiko yomphakathi mayelana nama-albhamu alindelwe isikhathi eside ikhombisa iphethini efanayo: abalaleli abeqa ngqo kuma-single bavame ukubuyela emuva ezinyangeni kamuva futhi bathole amathrekhi ajulile. Ngesikhathi sokudlala esiyihora elingu-1 nemizuzu engu-6, kunendawo eningi yokuthi intandokazi ekhula kancane ivele — "Imizamo," "Imidlwembe," no-"Umuntu Ongenacala" bonke bangabangani abalula bokuthi bakukhulise ngemva kokuyilalela okwesithathu noma okwesine.

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-albhamu ye-Sminofu iTekisi Spotify?**

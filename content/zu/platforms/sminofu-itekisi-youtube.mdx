## Amathiphu Asemthethweni Ukuze Uthole Okuningi Ku-Albhamu

Iphrojekthi enezingoma eziyi-17 izuza ekuhleleni okuncane. Le mikhuba yenza ukulalela kokuqala kube lula, noma ngabe uku-YouTube noma kuhlelo lokusakaza.

- **Lalela ngokulandelana kanye.** Isingeniso singu-1:28 futhi silungiselela ingoma eyinhloko ngqo. Ukushova kukulahla lokho.
- **Qala ngohlu olufushane.** Qala nge-"iTekisi," "Amalengiso," kanye no-"Ngithunjiwe," bese unweba. Kulula ukubeka izingoma ezijulile uma usuzazi izingoma ezidume kakhulu.
- **Gcina izingoma ezinde zohambo.** "Igeja" no-"Iyeke Leyondaba" yizona ezidinga isikhathi esikhulu kunazo zonke.
- **Hlola imininingwane yababambiqhaza ekhasini elisemthethweni.** Abaculi abahlanganyelayo kulula ukubakhohlwa ekulayishweni kwamavidiyo asusa imethadatha.
- **Landa noma gcina ukuze udlale ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.** Okulayishwe kwe-albhamu ezinkundleni zamavidiyo kungashabalala ngaphandle kwesixwayiso; uhlu lwezingoma olugciniwe ngeke lushabalale.
- **Qaphela usuku lokulayisha.** Noma yini efakwe ngaphambi kuka-Okthoba 16, 2026 akuyona le albhamu, noma ngabe isihloko sithini.

Kubafundi abathanda izithombe, amavidiyo amagama engoma ayisixazululo esifanelekile — kodwa thatha umsindo njengokubuka kuqala bese uqinisekisa ingxube yangempela enkundleni esemthethweni ngemuva kwalokho. Isipiliyoni sabasebenzisi sikhombisa ukuthi umehluko wekhwalithi yomsindo uyabonakala ezingomeni ezimatasatasa, ikakhulukazi lezo ezinabaculi abaningi.

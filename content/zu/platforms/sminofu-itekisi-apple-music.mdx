## Imibuzo Evame Ukubuzwa: Sminofu Itekisi Apple Music

**I-iTekisi kaSminofu yakhishwa nini?**
Le albhamu yakhishwa ngo-16 Okthoba 2026, nge-EMPIRE, futhi iyatholakala ukusakazwa ku-Apple Music ngalolo suku efika ngalo ku-iTunes Store.

**Zingaki izingoma eziku-iTekisi?**
Kunezingoma eziyi-17 ezithatha ihora elilodwa nemizuzu engu-6 isiyonke. Emfushane iyisingeniso semizuzu engu-1:28, i-"Benilindele Bani," kanti ende kunazo zonke i-"Igeja" ku-6:05.

**Ubani osesihambeli kule-albhamu?**
Abaculi abane bavela ezingomeni ezinhlanu: uLondeka Shangase ezingomeni ezimbili ("Ngithunjiwe" ne-"Ngo December"), kanye no-Ojakalasi, uNjabulo Langa, noZanefa Ngidi engomeni eyodwa umuntu ngamunye.

**Ngingalalela i-Sminofu Itekisi Apple Music ngaphandle kwaseNingizimu Afrika?**
Ukutholakala kuncike esitolo sakho. Uma i-albhamu ingaveli kukhathalogi yesifunda sakho, hlola isilungiselelo sezwe lakho ku-Apple Music noma ufune ukukhishwa ngaphansi kwesitolo sakho sendawo ngaphambi kokucabanga ukuthi ayitholakali.

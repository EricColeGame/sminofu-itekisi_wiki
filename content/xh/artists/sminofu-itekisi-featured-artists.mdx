## Kutheni Abaculi Abadibanayo kwi-iTekisi kaSminofu Ebalulekile

Qala ngamanani, kuba achaza iqhinga. *iTekisi* yafika ngo-16 Oktobha 2026, nge-Sminofu / EMPIRE, kwaye inengoma ezili-17 kwisithuba esimalunga neyure enye nemizuzu emithandathu. Zintlanu kuphela kwezo ngoma ezinekhredithi yomdwendwe — malunga nama-29% ecwecwe ngokwenani leengoma. Xa kulinganiswa ngexesha lokudlala, ezi ngoma zintlanu zidibanayo zidibanisa phantse imizuzu eli-17 nemizuzwana engama-49, okanye malunga nama-27% exesha lilonke lokudlala.

Olo kuzibamba lilo eyona nto ibalulekileyo. Icwecwe elineengoma ezili-17 linika umculi ithuba elininzi lokwabela abanye abaculi; uSminofu usebenzisa abaculi abane abadibanayo kwaye unika omnye kuphela ithuba lesibini. Isiphumo luluhlu lomculo apho ukuvela komdwendwe kusaziva njengesiganeko kunokuba kube yinto yokuzalisa.

## Into Eleyo Umdwendwe — Nendlela Yokuyiva

### ULondeka Shangase: UmDibanisi Ophindwe Kabini

UShangase ufumana elona xesha likhulu kunaye wonke umntu kwicwecwe, kwaye zombini iingoma zakhe zimfutshane. "Ngithunjiwe" ithatha 3:26, "Ngo December" ithatha 3:34 — akukho nanye egqitha umda wemizuzu emine. Oko kufutshane kuphakamisa ukucwangciswa okugxile kwikhorasi eyakhiwe malunga nokusebenzisana kunokuba kube kukudlala eyedwa ixesha elide. Mamela ezi ngoma zimbini zilandelana kwaye unokuva indlela ukubonakala kwesibini okukhupha ngayo umvakalo wokuqala ngaphandle kokuwuphinda.

### UOjakalasi: Isikhanyisi Saphakathi kwiCwecwe

"Emabhilidini" ithatha i-3:20, enye yeengoma ezimfutshane kakhulu kule rekhodi. Ibekwe kwingoma 9, ifika kanye emva kolona thotho lude lomculo wedwa kwaye ifaka utshintsho lwamandla kanye apho icwecwe elineengoma ezili-17 liqala khona ukufuna enye.

### UNjabulo Langa: Elona Thuba Lifutshane

"Umalumekazi" yeyona imfutshane kuzo zontlanu iingoma ezinabaculi abadibanayo nge-3:17. Oko kuyenza ibe yeyona ikhawulezayo, eyona yonga kakhulu intsebenziswano kwicwecwe — ukudluliselwa ngokukhawuleza kunokuba kube yingoma yokudlala ndawonye epheleleyo. Ukuba ufuna ukuqonda ukuba ingakanani indawo efunekayo ukuze umdwendwe ashiye uphawu, qala apha.

### UZanefa Ngidi: Isibambiso Sokugqibela seCwecwe

Yeyona ndawo yomdwendwe enendawo yokuphefumla, kwaye ilungiselela uthotho lokugqibela lomculo wedwa luka-"Ngo December," "Iyeke Leyondaba," no-"eMachobeni."

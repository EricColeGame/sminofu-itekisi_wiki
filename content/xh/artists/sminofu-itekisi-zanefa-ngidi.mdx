## Kutheni Ukukhangela i-Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi Kubalulekile

Ezinye iingoma zecwecwe zenzelwe iqhosha lokutsiba. Ezinye ziba sesona sizathu sokuba abantu bacinezele ukuqala ukuyimamela yonke irekhodi. Kubaphulaphuli abaninzi, unxulumano lukaSminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi lusemva kweli qela lesibini — ukubonakala kukandwendwe omnye okufihlakeleyo kwingoma ye-14 okuguqula icwecwe elide libe yincoko.

Ukuba ukhe wayokhangela elo binzana linamagama amathathu, kungenzeka ukuba waqala weva u-'Njengobaba' waza emva koko wayokhangela umxholo. Yile ndlela kanye esebenza ngayo iperi kaSminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi: ikhredithi imfutshane, kodwa umbono awunjalo. Esi sikhokelo sichaza ukuba ingoma ihleli phi kwicwecwe, ukuba iikhredithi zithini kanye, ukuba ukubonakala komntu ondwendwe kuhambelana njani nokuma kwecwecwe ngokubanzi, nendlela yokumamela ngendlebe engcono.

Uhlobo olufutshane: *iTekisi* yiprojekthi yeengoma ezili-17, kwaye ukubonakala kukaZanefa Ngidi yenye yezindawo ezine kuphela zeendwendwe kuyo. Ukuqonda loo ndawo kucacisa okuninzi malunga nendlela icwecwe eliyilwe ngayo.

## Ngubani uZanefa Ngidi, kwaye Kutheni u-'Njengobaba' Itsala

Lapha kulapho ukukhangela okuninzi kudana khona. Ingxelo yoluntu ngoZanefa Ngidi incinci — ukhredithiwe njengomculi ondwendwe ku-'Njengobaba,' kwaye loo khredithi yeyona ndawo ithembekileyo ekhoyo. Nantoni na engaphaya kwayo (i-discography epheleleyo, imbali yelebhile, uphawu lohlobo) ayibhalwanga kakuhle kwimithombo efikelelekayo, kwaye ukuphinda ibhayografi engaqinisekiswanga kuya kwenzakalisa abafundi.

Into enokuthethwa ngokuzithemba yileyo iboniswa yirekhodi ngokwayo:

- **Ukubonakala kuyintsebenziswano yokwenene yohlobo lweduet, hayi isampuli okanye ithegi yangasemva.** Ingoma idweliswe njengo-"Sminofu, Zanefa Ngidi," nto leyo iyifomathi yekhredithi esetyenziswa yiSpotify kwimidlalo yokwenene edibeneyo.
- **Ihamba i-4:12 epheleleyo.** Ukubonakala okuthatha ubude bengoma yonke kuziphatha ngendlela eyahlukileyo kwi-cameo yemizuzwana engama-20 — kuguqula isakhiwo sengoma, hayi nje umgangatho wayo.
- **Kufika emva kakhulu kulandelelwano.** Ukubonakala kweendwendwe emva kwecwecwe kudla ngokuba nomthwalo weemvakalelo kunomthwalo worhwebo. Kubekwa ukuze kusonjululwe into, hayi ukuthengisa isingle yokuqala.
- **Isihloko ngokwaso sinxulumene.** U-'Njengobaba' uthetha ngokurhabaxa "njengotata wam" ngesiZulu, nto leyo ebeka ingoma kwindawo yomnombo, ilifa, nokufana kosapho — indawo yendalo yamazwi amabini atshintshisanayo.

Elo phuzu lokugqibela yiyo le nto iperi kaSminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi ifundeka njengento engaphezu kwekhredithi. Ingoma eyakhiwe ngumfanekiso wotata imema indlela yokubiza nokuphendula, kwaye ifomathi yeduet inika umxholo iimbono ezimbini endaweni yeyone. Nokuba ingoma iyibhayografi okanye iyincoko, ukhetho lolungiselelo luhambelana nesihloko.

### Amava Abadlali Neengxelo Zoluntu

Phakathi kwabaphulaphuli abachithe ixesha nerekhodi, kukho imixholo embalwa ephindaphindiweyo. La ngamava abadlali neengxelo zoluntu, hayi idatha yeetshati eqinisekisiweyo, ngoko ke zithathe njengemibono kunokuba zibe ziinyani:

- Isiqingatha sokugqibela secwecwe sihlala sichazwa njengesinamandla ngakumbi kunesiqingatha sangaphambili, kwaye ukubonakala kweendwendwe kukhankanywa njengesizathu.
- U-"Igeja," kwi-6:05 eyona ngoma inde, uphawuleka njengento ekhulayo — kulula ukuyitsiba ekuqaleni, kunzima ukuyeka ukuyiphinda kamva.
- U-"Njengobaba" uhlala ebizwa njengencopho yeemvakalelo, kwaye iperi kaSminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi ihlala iyindawo yokungena yabalandeli abatsha abafike ngeekliphu kunokuba bafike ngecwecwe.
- Abanye abaphulaphuli baqaphela ukuba i-intro isebenza ngcono ngokulandelelana kunokuba ibe yingoma ezimeleyo, nto leyo eqhelekileyo kwiingoma ze-intro.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Yintoni kanye ekubhekiswa kuyo yi-"Sminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi"?**
Ibhekisa kwikhredithi ethile kwicwecwe likaSminofu lika-2026 *iTekisi*: ingoma ye-14, u-"Njengobaba," edlalwa nguSminofu kunye nomculi ondwendwe uZanefa Ngidi. La magama mathathu ayingoprojekthi ezahlukeneyo — achaza ingoma enye kwicwecwe elinye.

**Yeyiphi ingoma enoZanefa Ngidi kwiTekisi?**
Ingoma ye-14, u-"Njengobaba," ethatha imizuzu emi-4 nemizuzwana engama-12. Yeyona yesine kwaye inde kwiintsebenziswano zeendwendwe ezine zecwecwe, kwaye ihleli ngaphantsi kwemizuzu engama-50 kwirekhodi.

**Zingaphi iingoma ezikwiTekisi, kwaye inde kangakanani?**
Iingoma ezilishumi elinesixhenxe ezipheleleyo malunga neyure e-1 nemizuzu emi-6, ngobude obuqhelekileyo bengoma obumalunga nemizuzu emi-3 nemizuzwana engama-55. Eyona imfutshane yi-intro ye-1:28; eyona inde ngu-"Igeja" kwi-6:05.

**Ngaba u-"Njengobaba" ukhutshwe njengesingle ezimeleyo?**
Uluhlu olukhoyo lubonisa ukuba yingoma yecwecwe kwi-*iTekisi* kunokuba ibe sisingle ekhuthazwe ngokwahlukileyo. Ngokukodwa, u-"eMachobeni" — ingoma yokuvala yecwecwe — uvela njengokukhutshwa kwakhe kwi-discography kaSminofu, nto leyo ebonisa ukuba iqhinga leesingle lijolise kwenye indawo.

## Isishwankathelo

Umzuzu kaSminofu Itekisi Zanefa Ngidi yikhredithi encinci enendima enkulu. Ihleli nzulu kwicwecwe elineengoma ezili-17, ihamba ixesha elide kunayo nayiphi na intsebenziswano kwirekhodi, kwaye isihloko sayo siyibeka kwindawo yosapho nelifa — uhlobo lomxholo olunika umvuzo kulungiselelo lwamazwi amabini.

Ukuba uzele umxholo wendwendwe, hlala nolandelelwano. Qala kwingoma ye-14, emva koko ubuye umva ngo-"Imidlwembe" uze uqhubeke phambili ku-"Ngo December." Uya kuva ukuba kutheni indawo eyodwa yondwendwe kwicwecwe eliyimizuzu engama-66 yaba yinto abantu abayokhangela ngegama.

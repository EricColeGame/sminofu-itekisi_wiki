## Kutheni Intsebenziswano kaSminofu kwiTekisi Ibalulekile

Loo nani likuxelela ukuba loluphi uhlobo lwecwecwe: iingoma ezityunjiweyo zisebenza njengeziphumlisi, hayi njengolwakhiwo. Undwendwe ngalunye lufika emva kwexesha elide lomculi eyedwa luze lubuyisele icwecwe ngaphakathi kwemizuzu emine.

## Wonke Umculi Otyunjiweyo kwiTekisi

Uluhlu lweendwendwe lucwengekile ngokwaneleyo ukuba ungaluhambisa ngentloko. Nantsi inkcazo epheleleyo yentsebenziswano kaSminofu kwiTekisi, ngokulandelelana kwazo kwicwecwe.

### ULondeka Shangase — ekuphela komculi otyunjiweyo ophindayo

UShangase nguye kuphela umsebenzisani uSminofu amphindayo, kwaye ukuvela kwakhe kabini kubekwe kuhlukaniswe ziingoma ezilithoba: "Ngithunjiwe" kwingoma yesi-7 no "Ngo December" kwingoma yesi-15. Eso sikhewu sisekwe kulwakhiwo hayi kwinto nje eyenzekileyo. Uvula umjelo weendwendwe aze awuvale, nto leyo emenza abe ngumsonto odibanisa umbindi wecwecwe.

### U-Ojakalasi — ujiko phakathi kwicwecwe

"Emabhilidini" ifika kwingoma yesi-9, iphakathi kuka "Madagascar" (4:20) no "Es'thenjini" (4:12). Nge-3:20 yeyona imfutshane kwezi ngoma zintlanu zentsebenziswano, kwaye indawo yayo kanye embindini iyenza ibe sisitshixo secwecwe — indawo apho icwecwe lijika khona lisuka kwiingoma zokuqala zomculi eyedwa liye kwindawo yeendwendwe.

### UNjabulo Langa — eyona ndwendwe iqinileyo

"Umalumekazi" ithatha i-3:17, nto leyo eyenza ibe yeyesibini ngobufutshane kwiingoma ezipheleleyo kwiTekisi emva ko "Why Umehlulela" (3:12). Iindwendwe ezimfutshane zidla ngokuba luphawu lokuhlelwa okuxhomekeke kwi-hook, kwaye le ndawo — kanye emva ko "Imizamo" ongu-3:41 — igcina isiqingatha sokugqibela sihamba phambi kokuba kufike ezona ngoma zinde zimbini zentsebenziswano.

### UZanefa Ngidi — ujiko lwasemva kwicwecwe

"Njengobaba" yeyona inde kwiingoma zeendwendwe nge-4:12, kwaye ihleli kwingoma yesi-14, kwindawo enye phambi kokubuya kukaShangase. Iindwendwe ezilandelelanayo kwiingoma yesi-14 neyesi-15 zenza elona xesha lixineneyo lentsebenziswano kwicwecwe, kwaye lelo candelo lelona lifanele ukuviva njengombini kunokuba uve iingoma ezahlukeneyo.

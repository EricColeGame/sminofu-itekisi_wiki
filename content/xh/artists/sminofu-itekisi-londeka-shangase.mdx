Ukuba uye weva ii-single waza wazibuza ukuba iprojekthi epheleleyo iyabambana na, impendulo icacile: yebo — kodwa iinkcukacha zezo ezenza ukuba kube nexabiso ukuchitha ixesha layo. Ukwazi ukuba **iSminofu iTekisi** iqulathe ntoni, ngubani ondwendwe kuyo, nendlela ehambelana ngayo namarekhodi angaphambili kwenza ukumamela kwakho kokuqala okuhle kube kuhle kakhulu.

## Imibuzo eBuzwa Rhoqo

**Ikhutshwe nini iSminofu iTekisi?**

I-albhamu yakhutshwa nge-16 Oktobha 2026, phantsi kweSminofu / EMPIRE. Yandulelwa kwangalo nyaka ngo "eMachobeni" no "Woza Friday (Remake)," kwaye ingoma yayo yokugqibela inegama elifanayo nele "eMachobeni."

**Zingaphi iingoma ezikwiSminofu iTekisi?**

Kukho iingoma ezili-17 ezithatha malunga neyure enye nemizuzu emi-6. Zintlanu kuphela kwezo ngoma eziquka umculi ondwendwe, kwaye i-intro yeyona imfutshane nge-1:28.

**Ngubani uLondeka Shangase kule albhamu?**

ULondeka Shangase ngoyena mculi usebenza naye rhoqo kwiSminofu iTekisi, evela ku "Ngithunjiwe" (ingoma yesi-7) naku "Ngo December" (ingoma ye-15). Zombini ezi ngoma zikwiindawo ezicwangcisiweyo — enye kanye emva kwaphakathi, enye kwikota yokugqibela — nto leyo ezenza zibe ziindawo ezilula zokuqalisa kubamameliyo abatsha.

**Ndingayimamela phi i-albhamu epheleleyo?**

Irekhodi epheleleyo iyafumaneka kumaqonga amakhulu okusasaza, kuquka iSpotify, apho uluhlu lweengoma, ubude bazo, neekhredithi ziboniswa ngokupheleleyo. Ukuba ufuna ukuthelekisa i-albhamu namakhutshwa angaphambili, iphepha leprofayile yomculi lidwelisa lonke uluhlu lwemisebenzi endaweni enye.

**Kufanelekile ukuyimamela ngokulandelelana?**

Yebo. Kwiingoma ezili-17 kunye nesakhiwo esicwangcisiweyo — i-intro emfutshane, ingoma enegama le-albhamu kwangethuba, ingoma ende ephambili kwingoma yesihlanu, noluhlu olomeleleyo lokugqibela — ukuhamba kwexesha le-albhamu kuyinxalenye yamava. Ukuxuba iingoma ngokungacwangciswanga kuyayicima loo nto, ngakumbi kumamelo lokuqala.

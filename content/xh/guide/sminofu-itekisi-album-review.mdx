## Isandi: Ubuchule beMaskandi obunomdlandla wale mihla

Ukuveliswa kulo msebenzi kuhleli ngokukhululekileyo phakathi kwesiko leMaskandi kodwa kungafuni kuvakala njengento yakudala. Isiginci sisengumbalisi — imigca ecocekileyo edlalwa ngeminwe ephethe umculo phambi kokuba kuvele naliphi na ilizwi. Kuyo yonke indawo, isandi esiphantsi sizaliswe ngakumbi kunakwiirekhodi zakudala zeMaskandi, kwaye amagubu axutywe phezulu ngokwaneleyo ukuba iingoma zivakale kwisitiriyo semoto okanye kwisithethi sefowuni ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa yimo yazo.

Apho i-albhamu ibonisa ixabiso layo kukuzibamba. Akukho nto idityanisiweyo nje ukuzalisa indawo. Xa kuvela umgca weconcertina okanye accordion, uphendula isiginci endaweni yokukhuphisana naso, kwaye iqela labaculi lingena kuphela apho ulungiselelo lufuna ubunzima. Eso sisigqibo sokuvelisa, asiyonto yenzekileyo ngamabona, kwaye sesona sizathu sikhulu sokuba i-albhamu ihlale ihle xa iphindwa.

| Into | Indima kwi-albhamu | Yintoni omele uyiphulaphule |
|---|---|---|
| Isiginci esidlalwa ngeminwe | Siphethe umculo nesingqi ngaxeshanye | Ukujika okuncinci komculo phakathi kwemigca yelizwi |
| Concertina / accordion | Yonganza isandi kunye nombala wemveli | Indlela ephendula ngayo, ingasoloko igqithisa isiginci |
| Ibass namagubu | Zinika iingoma ubunzima bale mihla | Iipateni zekick eziqhubela ikhorasi phambili |
| Amalizwi eqela | Akha iinxalenye zokubiza nokuphendula | Umzuzu xa amalizwi angasemva ethe ngqo |
| Izixhobo zomculo ezidlalwa ngezandla nokuqhwaba | Zongeza amandla endawo yomdlalo | Iimpazamo ezincinci ezigcina umculo womntu |

Ukuba ufuna imvelaphi yesiko le-albhamu isuka kulo, [imbali nezixhobo zomculo zeMaskandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maskandi) lusixhobo esiluncedo sokuqala phambi kokuba uyidlale ngokupheleleyo okokuqala.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Ingaba i-albhamu yeSminofu Itekisi isiphumile?**
Iingxelo zoluntu zibonisa ukuba umsebenzi sele ukhutshiwe kwaye ujikeleza kumaqonga edijithali, kwaye iikliphu zivela rhoqo kwiividiyo ezimfutshane. Ukufumaneka kungahluka ngokwengingqi, ngoko jonga inkonzo yakho yokusasaza oyikhethayo ngqo.

**Ithetha ntoni ngokwenene "Itekisi"?**
Ligama lesiZulu elithetha *iteksi*. Kumxholo, lisebenza njengomfanekiso wokuhamba, ukuzabalaza, kunye nehlabathi elixineneyo lentlalo yemihla ngemihla yaseMzantsi Afrika.

**Ingaba le yindawo efanelekileyo yokuqala kumntu omtsha kwiMaskandi?**
Yebo. Amalungiselelo akhokelwa sisiginci ahlala ethembekile kuhlobo ngelixa ukuxuba kwale mihla kwenza i-albhamu ifikeleleke lula. Ukuba uyayonwabela, ikatalogi yangaphambili — kuquka iprojekthi ka-2023 ethi *Izitha* — yindawo elandelayo yendalo.

**Ihlala phi ngokubanzi le phononongo lwealbhamu yeSminofu Itekisi?**
Ngokuncomekayo. Ukubhala kubukhali ngakumbi kunokuba ii-hook zodwa zingacebisa, ukuveliswa kunethemba ngaphandle kokuxinana, kwaye ukusebenzisana kuyongeza endaweni yokuphazamisa. Luhlobo lwe-albhamu olufumana indawo esisigxina kumjikelo wokuphulaphula endaweni yokuqaqamba kweeveki ezimbini zokunikela ingqalelo.

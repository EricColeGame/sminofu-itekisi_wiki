## Iingcebiso ezisebenzayo zokufumana okungakumbi kolu luhlu lweengoma

Ukulazi uluhlu lweengoma lwe-Sminofu iTekisi sisiqingatha nje somsebenzi. Nazi iindlela zokusebenzisa ngokwenene:

1. **Gcina icwecwe njengoluhlu lweengoma phambi kokuba uqale.** Kwenza ukuhlela kwakhona ngokweendwendwe okanye ngokwexesha lokudlala kube lula kakhulu kunokutsala ifayile enye ende.
2. **Lungelelanisa ubude bengoma nexesha onalo lokumamela.** Iingoma ezisibhozo zingaphantsi kwemizuzu emine, ngoko ikhefu lemizuzu engama-20 lanele iingoma ezine okanye ezintlanu ezilungileyo.
3. **Sebenzisa iindwendwe njengesixhobo sokufumana umculo omtsha.** Ukuba iindwendwe zivakala kakuhle, khangela ikhatalogu yaloo mculi — yindlela abamameli abasoloko befumana ngayo umculo omtsha.
4. **Phinda uzimamele iingoma ezinde.** "Igeja" kwi-6:05 luhlobo lwengoma edla ngokuveza okungakumbi xa iphindwa, ngokweengxelo zoluntu.
5. **Qaphela umhla wokukhutshwa.** I-*iTekisi* yaphuma ngo-Oktobha 16, 2026, ngoko ke nantoni na ebhalwe ngaphambi kwaloo mhla ayiyi kubonisa ulandelelwano lokugqibela lweengoma.

Iingxelo zoluntu zibonisa ukuba icwecwe linomvuzo xa limanyelwa ukusuka ekuqaleni ukuya esiphelweni kunokuba lishukwe, ingakumbi kuba intshayelelo nengoma yokugqibela ziyilelwe ukuba zibonise umxholo wecwecwe liphela. Oko kungamava abamameli hayi ingxelo esemthethweni, kodwa kuhambelana nendlela uluhlu lweengoma olwakhiwe ngayo.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Zingaphi iingoma ezikuluhlu lweengoma lwe-Sminofu iTekisi?**
Zilishumi elinesixhenxe. Icwecwe livula nge-"Benilindele Bani (Intro)" kwaye livala nge-"eMachobeni," kwaye ixesha lilonke limalunga neyure enye nemizuzu emithandathu.

**Ngubani oyindwendwe kwi-iTekisi?**
Kukho iimvumi ezine kwiingoma ezintlanu. ULondeka Shangase ubalulwe kwi-"Ngithunjiwe" nakwi-"Ngo December," ngelixa uOjakalasi, uNjabulo Langa noZanefa Ngidi bevela kanye.

**Yeyiphi eyona ngoma inde kwicwecwe?**
"Igeja" kwi-6:05. Eyona imfutshane yintshayelelo yokuqala kwi-1:28, kwaye ubude obuqhelekileyo bengoma kuluhlu lweengoma lwe-Sminofu iTekisi limalunga nemizuzu emithathu nemizuzwana engama-55.

**Yakhutshwa nini iTekisi?**
Ngo-Oktobha 16, 2026, phantsi kwe-Sminofu / EMPIRE. Yalandela ukukhutshwa kwe-"eMachobeni" eyedwa kunye ne-"Woza Friday (Remake)" eyodwa kwangalo nyaka.

**Ingaba ingoma enesihloko yeyona ngoma yokuqala?**
Hayi. "iTekisi" yingoma yesibini, ilandela intshayelelo emfutshane. Ukuba ufuna ukuqala ngeyona ngoma imele icwecwe, qala apho kwaye ubuye kuyo intshayelelo kamva.

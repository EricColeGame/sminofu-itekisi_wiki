## Yintoni Ngokwenene "iTekisi"

*I-iTekisi* yingoma yesihloko nengoma yesibini kwi-albham kaSminofu ka-2026 enegama elifanayo, ithatha imizuzu emi-4:06. Igama elithi *itekisi* ligama lesiZulu elithetha iteksi — igama elithwele umhlaba uphela wentsingiselo kubomi bemihla ngemihla baseMzantsi Afrika. Iiranki zeeteksi, iindlela ezinde, nabalinganiswa abahamba kuzo yindawo eqhelekileyo yokubalisa amabali kwiMaskandi, uhlobo lwesiZulu olukhokelwa yigitare olwakhiwe phezu kwamabali, iingoma zokubiza nokuphendula, nokuqwalasela okubukhali kwintlalo.

Nokuba loo mifanekiso ise khona okanye ayikabi kho, isihloko sengoma sinika abaphulaphuli umfanekiso ocacileyo engqondweni ngaphambi kokuba kukhutshwe nomfanekiso omnye.

## Ngaba Ikhona Ividiyo Yomculo Esemthethweni kaSminofu iTekisi Okwangoku?

Oko akuthethi ukuba imifanekiso ayisokuze ivele; kuthetha ukuba abalandeli mabaqaphele ukulayishwa okungaqinisekiswanga de kube itshaneli esemthethweni ithumela enye.

Okudla ngokwenzeka ngengoma yesihloko yeMaskandi kucacile. Ingoma ikhutshwa kuqala ngokusasazwa, iintlu zokudlala ziyayithatha, kwaye imifanekiso ilandela xa irekhodi lifumene abaphulaphuli balo. I-*iTekisi* sele ibekwe kwiintlu zokudlala zokuhlela eziquka i-New Music Daily neMaskandi Hits, nto leyo edla ngokuba linqanaba apho ucwangciso-mali lwemifanekiso lunokwenzeka khona.

**Iingxelo zoluntu** ezivela kubalandeli abalandela ukukhutshwa zibonisa ukuba umsindo uye wasasazeka ngokubanzi ukususela phakathi ku-Oktobha, abaphulaphuli babelana ngeendinyana abazithandayo kunee kliphu. De kuvele ukulayishwa okusemthethweni, eyona ngcamango ikhuselekileyo kukuba nayiphi na ividiyo "esemthethweni" odibana nayo ilayishwe kwakhona kusetyenziswa umfanekiso wealbham njengomfanekiso ongashukiyo.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Ngaba ikhona ividiyo yomculo esemthethweni kaSminofu iTekisi ngoku?**
Akukho vidiyo isemthethweni eqinisekisiweyo ekwaziqinisekisayo kwiinkcukacha zokukhutshwa ezihambelana nealbham. Okukhutshiweyo okubhaliweyo yi-albham yokusasazwa eneengoma ezili-17, ngoko ke nayiphi na ividiyo ethi isemthethweni kufuneka iqwalaselwe kwiitshaneli eziqinisekisiweyo zikaSminofu ne-EMPIRE phambi kokuba uyithembe.

**Ingoma "iTekisi" inde kangakanani?**
Ingoma yesihloko ithatha imizuzu emi-4:06 kwaye ikwindima yesibini kwi-albham, ngokukhawuleza emva kwentshayelelo ethi "Benilindele Bani" yemizuzu engu-1:28.

**Ndingayisasaza phi ialbham ngokusemthethweni?**
Ialbham epheleleyo iyafumaneka kwi-Apple Music nakwi-iTunes Store, ibhalwe phantsi kukaSminofu kunye neleyibhile ye-EMPIRE. Ukusasaza apho yeyona ndlela ithembekileyo yokuva umculo osemthethweni.

**Ndenze ntoni ukuba ndifumana ividiyo yomculo kaSminofu iTekisi ekhangeleka ingeyonyani?**
Jonga itshaneli ukuba inebheji yoqinisekiso, qinisekisa ukuba inkcazo ibalula uSminofu okanye i-EMPIRE, kwaye thelekisa ixesha ne-4:06. Ukuba enye yazo ayiphumelelanga, xela ukulayishwa kwayo kwaye uqhubeke ujonga iitshaneli ezisemthethweni — kulapho ukukhutshwa kwangempela kuya kuvela khona kuqala.

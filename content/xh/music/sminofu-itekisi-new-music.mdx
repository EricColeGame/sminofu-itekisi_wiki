## Okuqulathwe nguMculo Omtsha kaSminofu iTekisi Ngo-2026

USminofu ungenelela kulo mkhuba ngaphandle kokuwuthatha njengento yemyuziyam. Icwecwe licwangciswe njengomdlalo wangqo: intshayelelo emfutshane efana nentetho, emva koko uthotho lweengoma ezipheleleyo ezitshintshana phakathi kokucinga nesingqisho.

Ingoma enesihloko ikwindawo yesibini kulandelelwano, nto leyo engaqhelekanga kwicwecwe elithiywe ngegama lomnye wengoma yalo. Ukubeka u-"iTekisi" kwangoko kangaka kubonisa ukuzithemba — uSminofu akawugcini umxholo ophambili de kube sekupheleni. Intshayelelo, "Benilindele Bani," ithatha nje u-1:28, isebenza njengomnyango kunokuba ibe yingoma epheleleyo.

Uluhlu lweengoma zexesha leeholide, imicimbi yomtshato, neendibano zosapho konke kuqala ukwakheka ngasekupheleni kwentwasahlobo, kwaye icwecwe elikhutshwa phakathi kuOktobha linamaviki amalunga neshumi lokuzinza phambi kukaDisemba. Iqela likaSminofu liyayiqonda ngokucacileyo le khalenda. Icwecwe elineengoma ezili-17 linika oo-DJ nabaqulunqi boluhlu lweengoma umxholo owaneleyo wokuqhubeka bejikelezisa umculi omnye iinyanga ngaphandle kokuphinda ingoma.

- **"iTekisi" (ingoma 2)** — ingoma enesihloko nesibhengezo esicacileyo seenjongo. Imizuzu emine, akukho mculi undwendwe, akukho kuzalisa.

**Mamela ngamahlandlo amabini.** Qala ngeengoma 1 ukuya ku-9, emva koko 10 ukuya ku-17. Icwecwe linendawo yephakathi yendalo malunga no-"Emabhilidini," kwaye ukwahlula apho kugcina iimvumi eziziiindwendwe zingadibani.

**Yenza uluhlu olufutshane lweengoma kuqala.** Khetha iingoma ezintlanu, hlala nazo iveki, emva koko buyela kwezinye. Amava abaphulaphuli kuluntu lomculo olude ahlala ebonisa ukuba iicwecwe ezimanyelwa ngokucothayo zihlala zijikeleziswa ixesha elide kunezo zimanyelwe kanye.

## Isigqibo ngoMculo Omtsha kaSminofu iTekisi

*iTekisi* licwecwe leMaskandi elizithembileyo nelilandelelaniswe kakuhle, eliqonda imveli yalo nokusasazwa kwalo. Ubude beeingoma ezili-17 buhlonipha okulindelwe luhlobo, umndilili ongaphantsi kwemizuzu emine liyigcina ilungele ukusasazwa, kwaye iimvumi eziziiindwendwe zibekwe apho ziphakamisa icwecwe kunokuba ziyiphatha. Umculo omtsha kaSminofu iTekisi awuyi kuguqula nabani na ongazange adibane nomsebenzi wesiginci weMaskandi, kodwa kubaphulaphuli abasele bengaphakathi kolu hlobo — okanye abanomdla kulo — le yenye yezona ngxelo zipheleleyo zonyaka.

Iingxelo zoluntu namava abaphulaphuli ekugqibeleni ziya kugqiba ukuba zeziphi kwezi ngoma eziza kuba ziingoma eziqhelekileyo emitshatweni nasemadibini. Ngokulandela ulwakhiwo lodwa, u-"Ngithunjiwe," u-"Imizamo," no-"Ngo December" babonakala ngathi ngabagqatswa bokuqala.

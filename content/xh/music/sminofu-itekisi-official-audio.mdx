Amanqanaba ahlawulelwayo asusa iintengiso kwaye avumela ukukhuphela ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.

**Ide kangakanani ingoma yesihloko?**
"iTekisi" ithatha u-4:06 kwaye ikwingoma yesibini, ngqo emva kwesingeniso esingu-1:28 esithi "Benilindele Bani." Ukuba inguqulelo oyifumeneyo ibonisa ixesha elahluke kakhulu, kusenokwenzeka ukuba ilulayisho lwakhona okanye uhlelo olulungisiweyo.

**Kutheni ndingayifumani i-audio esemthethweni ye-Sminofu Itekisi kwiiplatfomu zevidiyo?**

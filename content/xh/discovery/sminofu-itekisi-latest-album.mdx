## Okunikezelwa Yi-albhamu Yamva Nje kaSminofu iTekisi

USminofu wakha *iTekisi* njengesitatimende esipheleleyo kunokuba abe yiqela leengoma ezizodwa. Icwecwe livula ngesingeniso esifutshane, "Benilindele Bani," esithatha ngaphantsi kwemizuzu engamashumi alithoba kwaye sidlulela ngqo kwingoma enegama le-albhamu kwindawo yesibini. Ukusuka apho liqhubela phambili kwezinye iingoma ezilishumi elinesine phambi kokuba livalwe nge-"eMachobeni."

Olo landelelwano lukhetho olwenziwe ngabom. Isingeniso esidlulela ngqo kwingoma enegama le-albhamu sikuxelela ukuba i-albhamu inolandelelwano olucwangcisiweyo, kwaye sesona sigqibo sinomdla kwisakhiwo se-**albhamu yamva nje kaSminofu iTekisi**. Kwixesha apho uninzi lwabaphulaphuli luthatha ingoma enye ngexesha, iprojekthi eyakhelwe ukuphulaphulwa ngokulandelelana iyahluka.

*iTekisi* ayilona ukukhutshwa okuzimeleyo. USminofu ebesakha uluhlu lweengoma ngokuthe chu ukusukela ngo-2023, kwaye ngo-2026 kuphela kuye kwavelisa amangeno amaninzi. Ukubona uluhlu lokukhutshwa ecaleni kucacisa umlinganiselo we-**albhamu yamva nje kaSminofu iTekisi**.

Kuvela iipateni ezimbini. Okokuqala, uSminofu ukhupha rhoqo, nto leyo ethetha ukuba *iTekisi* isebenza njengesishwankathelo sendlela isandi esihleli ngayo ngoku kunokuba ibe luvavanyo olukodwa. Okwesibini, uluhlu lwabaculi abadityanisiweyo luhleli luncinci kuzo zonke iiprojekthi — *Choice Yethu* ixhomekeke kumculi omnye ondwendwe, kwaye *iTekisi* isasaza iindwendwe ezine kwiingoma ezilishumi elinesixhenxe. Ukungaguquguquki kwabo bamenyiweyo yinxalenye yesazisi.

Ukuba ufika kuSminofu ngaleli cwecwe, elona nyathelo eliluncedo elilandelayo kukuthelekisa ingoma enegama le-albhamu ye-*iTekisi* neengoma ezinamagama e-*Rush Hour* ne-*Sajola Kamnandi*. Umahluko kubuninzi bemveliso yomculo uya kukuxelela ngakumbi ngendlela umculi ahamba ngayo kunokuba naluphi na uphononongo.

Iyure luluzibophelelo lokwenene, ngoko ke kuyanceda ukuba nesicwangciso. Indlela engezantsi yahlula icwecwe ngokwamaxesha alawulekayo ngaphandle kokuphula ulandelelwano olucwangcisiweyo.

Iingcebiso ezimbalwa ezisebenzayo:

- **Sebenzisa ii-headphones ezilungileyo kwisingeniso.** "Benilindele Bani" yi-1:28 kuphela, kodwa ilungiselela ingoma enegama le-albhamu kwaye kulula ukuyitsiba ngempazamo.
- **Musa ukuxuba isiqingatha sokuqala.** Ukudluliselwa kwisingeniso ukuya kwingoma enegama le-albhamu sesona sigqibo singcono solandelelwano kwicwecwe.
- **Nika iingoma ezinde ixesha lokwenyani.** "Igeja" kwi-6:05 ne-"Iyeke Leyondaba" kwi-5:11 zifuna ingqalelo; zilahleka kakhulu xa zidlalwa ngasemva.

- **Yigcinele ekugqibeleni ingoma yokugqibela.** "eMachobeni" isebenza ngenxa yokuba ilandela i-"Iyeke Leyondaba," hayi ngenxa yokuba izimele yodwa.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Yaphuma nini i-albhamu yamva nje kaSminofu iTekisi?**
*iTekisi* yakhutshwa ngo-Okthobha 16, 2026, kunye namalungelo abhalwe kuSminofu / EMPIRE. Yeyona projekthi ipheleleyo yamva nje kuluhlu lomculi ukuza kuthi ga ngoku.

**Zingaphi iingoma ezikwi-iTekisi?**
Zili-17 iingoma zizonke, zithatha malunga neyure enye nemizuzu emi-6. Eyona imfutshane yi-1:28 yesingeniso, "Benilindele Bani," kwaye eyona inde ngu-"Igeja" kwi-6:05.

**Ngubani ondwendwe kwi-albhamu yamva nje kaSminofu iTekisi?**
Kukho abaculi abane abavela kwiingoma ezine: uLondeka Shangase ku-"Ngithunjiwe" naku-"Ngo December," uOjakalasi ku-"Emabhilidini," uNjabulo Langa ku-"Umalumekazi," noZanefa Ngidi ku-"Njengobaba."

**Zeziphi iingoma ekufanele ndiqale ngazo?**
Qala ngengoma enegama le-albhamu "iTekisi," emva koko zama "Ngithunjiwe" ngendlela iindwendwe ezisebenza ngayo kunye ne-"Iyeke Leyondaba" ngocwangciso olude. Ukuba uzonwabela ezo zintathu, ulandelelwano olupheleleyo lweengoma ezilishumi elinesixhenxe lufanele ixesha leyure.

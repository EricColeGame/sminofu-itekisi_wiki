## Iingcebiso Ezisebenzayo Zokusasaza i-Sminofu iTekisi Ngcono

Imikhwa embalwa emincinci yenza amava okumamela abe lula, ngakumbi kwidatha yeselula.

- **Khuphela phambi kokuba uhambe.** Gcina icwecwe elipheleleyo lemizuzu engama-66 kwi-Wi-Fi kwaye uphephe ukulinda xa usendleleni.
- **Sebenzisa iqhosha lokubonela kuqala.** Ukubonela kuqala kwe-Apple Music kukuvumela ukuba uve ingoma nganye phambi kokuba unikezele indawo yokukhuphela.
- **Yakha uluhlu lweeNgoma ezineendwendwe.** Hlanganisa iingoma ezintlanu ezinendwendwe kuluhlu olunye lwemizuzu eli-17 yezona zibalaseleyo.
- **Landela iphepha lomculi.** Ii-single ezintsha nokufakwa kuluhlu lweeNgoma kubonakala apho kuqala, kwaye yileyo ndlela uninzi lwabalandeli olufumana ngayo iindaba.
- **Jonga isetingi somgangatho weaudio.** Ukusasaza okukwinqanaba eliphezulu kuvakala ngcono kakhulu kumsebenzi wegitala oneengqimba we-Maskandi, kodwa kusebenzisa idatha eninzi.
- **Qeqesha ingoma eyisihloko ne-"Igeja."** Ukuthelekisa ezi zimbini kukunika umbono okhawulezayo ngoluhlu lwecwecwe, ukusuka kwi-single emfutshane ukuya kumculo owandisiweyo.

Ukuba umamela kakhulu kwi-speaker yeselula, ii-headphone ziya kutshintsha amava ngaphezu kwaso nasiphi na isetingi se-equalizer — imigca yegitala ekhutshwa ngeminwe echaza i-Maskandi kulula ukuyilahleka kwigumbi elinengxolo.

## Imibuzo Ebuzwa Rhoqo

**Ngaba iTekisi ka-Sminofu iyafumaneka ukuba isasazwe ngoku?**
Ewe. Icwecwe lakhutshwa ngo-Oktobha 16, 2026, phantsi kwe-EMPIRE, kwaye uluhlu olupheleleyo lweeNgoma ezili-17 luyasebenza kwi-Apple Music kunye nokubonela kuqala. Amanye amaqonga nawo ayalandela, kodwa ukufumaneka kunokwahluka ngokwelizwe, ngoko khangela ngegama lomculi ukuba ikhonkco elithe ngqo alilayishi.

**Lide kangakanani icwecwe, kwaye lineengoma ezingaphi?**
iTekisi ineengoma ezili-17 kwaye ithatha iyure e-1 nemizuzu emi-6, okuphakathi malunga nemizuzu emi-3 nemizuzwana engama-55 ngengoma. Eyona imfutshane yi-intro engu-1:28; eyona inde ngu-"Igeja" kwi-6:05.

**Ngabo banani iingcali ezindwendwe kwi-iTekisi?**
Iindwendwe ezine zivela kwiingoma ezintlanu: uLondeka Shangase ku-"Ngithunjiwe" naku-"Ngo December," u-Ojakalasi ku-"Emabhilidini," uNjabulo Langa ku-"Umalumekazi," noZanefa Ngidi ku-"Njengobaba."

**Yeyiphi eyona ndlela ilungileyo yokuqala ukusasaza i-Sminofu iTekisi okokuqala?**
Dlala ingoma eyisihloko, emva koko u-"Igeja," emva koko ingoma kaLondeka Shangase ethi "Ngithunjiwe." Olo landelelwano lweeNgoma ezintathu lubonisa uluhlu lwecwecwe kwimizuzu eli-14 kwaye lukuxelela ngokukhawuleza ukuba ufuna ukumamela yonke into ukusuka ekuqaleni ukuya esiphelweni.

**Ngaba icwecwe line-intro endingayitsiba?**
Ungayitsiba, kodwa i-"Benilindele Bani (Intro)" ibeka umoya ngaphantsi kwemizuzwana engamashumi asithoba kwaye ingena ngqo kwingoma eyisihloko, ngoko uninzi lwabaphulaphuli luyigcina indaweni yayo kudlalo lokuqala.

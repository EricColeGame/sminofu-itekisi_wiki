## Igeja: Kutheni le Ngoma yeMizuzu eMithandathu iyiSiseko seSminofu Itekisi Igeja

"Igeja" yingoma yesihlanu, ithatha u-6:05, kwaye yeyona ngoma inde kule albhamu — ngemizuzwana engama-54 ngaphezu kwengoma elandelayo eyona inde. Oko kukodwa bekuya kuyenza ibonakale, kodwa indawo ekuyo yenza umsebenzi ongaphezulu kwexesha lokudlala. Xa ifika, sele uvile intshayelelo ye-1:28, ingoma enesihloko yemizuzu emine, "Amalengiso," no-"Why Umehlulela." Icwecwe sele limisele isantya salo. Emva koko "Igeja" iyacothisa yonke into.

Iingoma ezinde aziyonto ingaqhelekanga kwiMaskandi. Amacwangciso aloo hlobo akhokelwa sisiginci ngokwesiko ashiya indawo yeentshayelelo ezinde, iindinyana ezithethwayo, namabinzana ezwi aphindaphindwayo akha uxinzelelo endaweni yokungxama ukuya kwikhorasi. Ngoko ke ingoma yemizuzu emithandathu ayiyomboniso wamabhongo kodwa kukubuyela kwimo — uhlobo lwengoma olunika umvuzo umphulaphuli ongatsibiyo phambili.

Kuye nabani na ofike ngokukhangela **Sminofu Itekisi Igeja**, isifundo esisebenzayo silula: le yingoma yokuvavanya icwecwe. Ukuba ulwakhiwo lwemizuzu emithandathu luyakubamba ingqalelo, nayo yonke enye icwecwe iya kukubamba. Ukuba ukhetha iingoma ezimfutshane neziqala ngehuku, qala ngengoma enesihloko okanye "Amalengiso" endaweni yayo, emva koko uqhubeke.

Iingxelo zoluntu malunga neengoma ezinde zeMaskandi zidla ngokuvakalisa umbono omnye — ezi ngoma azifane ziziveze ngokupheleleyo kudlalo olunye. Intshayelelo, utshintsho olusembindini wengoma, nokuphindaphinda kokugqibela ngokwahlukeneyo ziyavakala kudlalo lwesibini okanye lwesithathu.

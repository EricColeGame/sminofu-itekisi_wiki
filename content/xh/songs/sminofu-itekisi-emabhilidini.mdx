## Indlela i-Emabhilidini Elingana Ngayo nokulandelelana kwe-Albhamu

Ukulandelelana kweengoma kukho apho i-albhamu ende iphumelela okanye ilahleka khona, kwaye **iTekisi** ihlelwe ngenjongo ecacileyo. Intshayelelo imfutshane — imizuzwana engama-88 — kwaye idlulisela ngokukhawuleza kwingoma enesihloko yemizuzu emine. Ukusuka apho, uSminofu atshintshe phakathi kweengoma zokucinga neengoma ezihamba phambili ngesigqi, kwaye iindawo zabaculi abadibeneyo zihlukaniswe phantse zibe ziingoma ezimbini okanye ezintathu, hayi ukuba zidityaniswe ndawonye.

Njengoko ibekwe njengengoma yokugqibela yelo qela lokuqala labadibeneyo, "Emabhilidini" isebenza njengendawo yokuphumla. Ingoma emfutshane yemizuzu emithathu nemizuzwana engama-20 phakathi kweengoma ezimbini ezinde zomculi yedwa inika indlebe utshintsho lwendlela kanye ngexesha elifanelekileyo — into efanele ukuyiqaphela xa uziva i-Sminofu Itekisi Emabhilidini kumxholo wayo.

Kwabakha uluhlu lweengoma, oko kukwathetha ukuba ingoma ihamba kakuhle kumjikelo omfutshane. Yiyo ingoma eyomeleleyo ezimeleyo kodwa iphulukana nomoya omncinane xa ihlukaniswa noluhlu lweengoma 7 ukuya ku-10.

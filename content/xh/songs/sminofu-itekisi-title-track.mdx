## Kutheni Ingoma Eyintloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi Ibalulekile

Iingoma ezintloko ze-albham zinomsebenzi othile: zizisa ukuba icwecwe limalunga nantoni phambi kokuba umphulaphuli anikele ngeyure yakhe. Kwi-*iTekisi*, lo msebenzi uwela kwingoma eyintloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi — ingoma yesibini kwiprojekthi eneengoma ezili-17 eyafika ngo-Okthobha 16, 2026, nge-Sminofu ne-EMPIRE. Ithatha imizuzu emine nemizuzwana emithandathu, ayinamculi otyelelweyo, kwaye ihleli ngqo emva kwentshayelelo ye-1:28. Loo ndawo icwangciswe ngabom. Intshayelelo iyalungisa; ingoma eyintloko yeyona nto yokuqala yokwenyani.

Ukuba ubuzama ukuqonda ukuba yintoni kanye ingoma eyintloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi, indawo ekuyo kuluhlu lweengoma, nokuba i-albham eyingqongileyo ikufanele na ukuchitha ubusuku bakho, esi sikhokelo sigubungela uluhlu lweengoma, umxholo, neengcebiso ezimbalwa zokuphulaphula.

Ngamafutshane: yeyona ngoma iyintloko kwicwecwe elide, yenye yezona zinto zibalulekileyo ezenziwa ngumculi eyedwa, kwaye yeyona ndlela ingcono yokuqala ukuba ufuna ukuqonda into eyenziwa yi-*iTekisi* phambi kokuba uphulaphule yonke imizuzu engama-66.

## Into Ekufuneka Uyiphulaphule Kwingoma Eyintloko ye-Sminofu Itekisi

### Igama ngokwalo

*iTekisi* ligama lesiZulu elithetha "iteksi," kwaye i-albham iboleka eso sakhelo kwigama layo. Loo nto ngumfanekiso otyebileyo kumxholo waseMzantsi Afrika — ukuhamba ngokwabelwana, ukuhamba imihla ngemihla, iincoko phakathi kwabantu ongabaziyo — kwaye inika ingoma eyintloko umxholo okhoyo wokudlala ngawo. Nokuba ingoma isebenzisa isikweko ngokoqobo okanye iyithatha njengemvakalelo, ukwazi inguqulelo kutshintsha indlela amazwi avakala ngayo.

### Ulwimi nendlela yokuthetha

Uluhlu lweengoma lwakhiwe phantse ngokupheleleyo ngezihloko zesiZulu, kwaye ingoma eyintloko ilandela loo pateni. Ukuba awuthethi isiZulu, musa ukuvumela oko kukuthintele: umculo nesingqi zithwala inxalenye enkulu yentsingiselo, kwaye iinguqulelo zamazwi ziyafumaneka kumapulatifomu okusasaza nakuluntu lwabalandeli. Ukuphulaphula okwesibini okanye okwesithathu uvule inguqulelo kufanelekile ngokwenene.

### Ubude nokuhamba kwexesha

Kwi-4:06, ingoma inendawo yentshayelelo efanelekileyo, ubuncinane iindawo ezimbini ezipheleleyo zokucula, nesiphelo esingabonakali ngathi siyangxamela. Yithelekise ne-"Why Umehlulela" ekwi-3:12, eyakhelwe ukunika amandla ngokukhawuleza, kwaye unokuva umahluko wamabhongo phakathi kwengoma eyintloko nengoma efihlakeleyo.

### Uluhlu lweengcebiso ezisebenzayo zokuphulaphula

- **Phulaphula kube kanye ngaphandle kokuphazamiseka.** Musa ukuskrola, musa ukutsiba. Ingoma ivuza umvuzo wengqalelo ngakumbi kunokudlalwa ngasemva.
- **Phulaphula okwesibini unamazwi okanye inguqulelo.** Intsingiselo ifika emva kwexesha kumculo omninzi wolwimi lwesiZulu ukuba awuthethi ngokutyibilikayo.
- **Yidlale ngokulandelelana kwe-albham.** Ukudlula kwintshayelelo ye-1:28 ukuya kwingoma eyintloko yinxalenye yamava.
- **Yithelekise nengoma yesithathu.** "Amalengiso" ekwi-4:10 iphantse ilingane ngobude, nto leyo eyenza esi sibini sibe sisifundo esiluncedo sokuba uSminofu atshintsha njani isimo sengqondo ngomlinganiselo ofanayo wesantya.
- **Emva koko tsibela kwiingoma ezinabanye abaculi.** Iingoma 7, 9, 12, ne-15 zibonisa icala elahlukileyo lecwecwe.

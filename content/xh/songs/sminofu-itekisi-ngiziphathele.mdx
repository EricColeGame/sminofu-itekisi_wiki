## Kutheni i-Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele Ikwi-Playlist yoMlandeli ngamnye weMaskandi

Abaphulaphuli beMaskandi abakhangela **Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele** balandela enye yezinto ezixoxwa kakhulu kwikhalenda yomculo waseMzantsi Afrika. Icwecwe likaSminofu *iTekisi* lafika ngo-Okthobha 16, 2026, kwaye kwiintsuku nje ezimbalwa ibinzana **Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele** laba yindlela eqhelekileyo abalandeli abayibhalayo xa befuna ukufikelela kwicwecwe — inxalenye yesihloko secwecwe, inxalenye yomgcini wengoma ekhunjulwayo, nenxalenye nje umdla. Kutheni oko kubalulekile? Kuba *iTekisi* asilona cwecwe lokukhawuleza elikhutshwayo. Luludwe olupheleleyo: iingoma ezili-17, malunga neyure enye nemizuzu emithandathu yamabali eMaskandi, ekhutshwe nge-EMPIRE.

Oko kukala akwenzeki rhoqo. Uninzi lweecwecwe ezizimeleyo zeMaskandi ziba neengoma ezisibhozo ukuya kwezilishumi elinesibini; iprojekthi eneengoma ezili-17 ibonisa umculi onento yokuthetha kunye neleyibhile ekulungeleyo ukumxhasa. Kubalandeli bexesha elide bolu hlobo nakubantu abatsha abafike kwicwecwe ngekliphu emfutshane, icwecwe linikezela ngomxube onqabileyo weMaskandi wesiqhelo okhokelwa yigitare kunye nokugqitywa kwale mihla, okulungele unomathotholo.

Esi sikhokelo sicazulula ukuba *iTekisi* iqulathe ntoni ngokwenene, zeziphi iingoma ezifanele ukuphulaphulwa kuqala, ngubani ovela njengondwendwe, nendlela yokufumana okungcono kakhulu kwicwecwe xa uliphulaphula okokuqala. Ukuba ufike apha ngokuchwetheza u-Sminofu Itekisi Ngiziphathele kwibar yokukhangela, thatha oku njengolwazi olupheleleyo lokuqala.

### Apho u-'Ngiziphathele' angena khona

Into enye ekufanele uyicacise kwangoko: uluhlu olusemthethweni lweengoma ezili-17 alukho ingoma enesihloko esithi "Ngiziphathele." Oku kuthetha ukuba ibinzana elikwibar yokukhangela mhlawumbi liqhekeza lomgcini wengoma, umbhalo womlandeli, okanye isihloko esikhunjulwe ngokungachanekanga esatheleleka kwicwecwe kwi-intanethi. Oku kwenzeka rhoqo kwiicwecwe zeMaskandi, apho umgca omnye ophindwayo kwikhorasi unokuhamba kude kunegama elisemthethweni lengoma. Ngoko ukuba ukhangela ingoma ethile, uluhlu olupheleleyo lweengoma olungezantsi yeyona ndlela ikhawulezayo yokuyifumana.

Ukwenziwa komculo, okwenziwe ngokuxhaswa yi-EMPIRE, kugcina iigitare ziphambili kumxube ngelixa kunika indawo eyaneleyo kwizandi eziphantsi nakwizigubhu ukuze zivakale kwiisipika zefowuni — nto leyo, kubaphulaphuli abaninzi baseMzantsi Afrika, kulapho icwecwe livavanywa khona ngokwenene.

Iipateni ezimbalwa ziyavela kwiingoma ezili-17:

- **Indawo njengomxholo.** "Madagascar," "Es'thenjini," no "eMachobeni" zonke zalatha kwiindawo, isixhobo sakudala seMaskandi sokumisa ingoma kwindawo yokwenene.

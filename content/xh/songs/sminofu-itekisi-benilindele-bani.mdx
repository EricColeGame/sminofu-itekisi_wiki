## Ithini intsingiselo ye-"Benilindele Bani"?

Isihloko sesiZulu, kwaye ukusahlula ngokweengxenye kuyanceda. Ingcambu *-lind-* ithwala umxholo wokulinda okanye ukujonga into ethile, ngelixa *bani* ligama lombuzo "ngubani." Xa zifundwa kunye, ibinzana lidla ngokuqondwa ngabaphulaphuli njengombuzo ofana no "Umlindele bani?"

| Icandelo | Into eliyongezelelayo |
|---|---|
| Benilindele | Yakhiwe kwingcambu yokulinda, enophawu lomntu wesibini kwisininzi — malunga no "nilindele" |
| Bani | "Ngubani" — uguqula isivakalisi sibe ngumbuzo |
| (Intro) | Ibonisa umsebenzi womculo njengowokuqala kunokuba ube yingoma ezimeleyo |
| iTekisi | Isihloko secwecwe; igama lesiZulu leteksi encinci yomthutho |

Olo fundo lubalulekile kuba lutshintsha indlela ova ngayo lonke icwecwe. Icwecwe eliqala ngokubuza ukuba umlindele bani, kwiprojekthi ethiywe ngezothutho lukawonke-wonke, likumema ukuba ucinge ngendawo oya kuyo, ukulibaziseka, nokuba ngubani ohamba nawe. Olu lutoliko kunokuba lube yingxelo esemthethweni yenjongo — akukho nguqulelo ipapashiweyo ehamba nokukhutshwa — kodwa luphindaphindayo phakathi kwabaphulaphuli abaxoxa nge-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani.

## Imibuzo ebuzwa rhoqo

**Ngubani ocula "Benilindele Bani"?**
USminofu uyenza yedwa le ngoma. Kungumculo wokuqala kwi-*iTekisi* kwaye kuphela kwengoma ekwicwecwe engomculi odityanisiweyo uqinisekisiweyo.

**Ithatha ixesha elingakanani i-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani?**
Ithatha u-1:28, nto leyo eyenza ibe ngowona mculo mfutshane kwicwecwe ngomgama omkhulu — owesibini ngobufutshane, "Why Umehlulela," ngu-3:12.

**Ithini intsingiselo yesihloko ngesiNgesi?**
Ibinzana lidla ngokuqondwa njengo "Umlindele bani?" Lidityanisa ingcambu yesenzi sesiZulu enxulumene nokulinda negama lombuzo *bani*. Akukho nguqulelo isemthethweni epapashiweyo, ngoko oku kuselulutoliko.

**Ingaba i-Sminofu Itekisi Benilindele Bani iyafumaneka njengengoma ezimeleyo?**
Akukho qinisekiso yokukhutshwa kwengoma ezimeleyo kwimathiriyeli ekhoyo. Okwangoku, eyona ndlela ilungileyo yokuyiva ngumculo wokuqala we-*iTekisi*, okhutshwe ngo-16 Oktobha 2026.

**Kutheni intshayelelo yemizuzwana engama-88 ibalulekile?**
Kuba yinto yokuqala umphulaphuli ayivayo kwicwecwe elineengoma ezili-17. Imisa umbuzo ongundoqo wesihloko, idlulisela ngqo kwingoma enesihloko, kwaye ikubiza ngaphantsi komzuzu nesigamu — utyalo-mali oluncinci ukuze ube nombono ocace ngakumbi woko ukwenzayo icwecwe liphelele.

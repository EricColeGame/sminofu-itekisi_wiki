## Kutheni Wonke Umntu Ekhangela i-Sminofu Itekisi ku-YouTube

Olo khangelo lunzima kunokuba lubonakala — le projekthi ineengoma ezili-17 kwaye ithatha malunga neyure enye, kwaye iziphumo zevidiyo zayo zingumxube weaudio enelayisensi, iikliphu zamazwi eengoma, nokulayishwa kwakhona ngabalandeli. Esi sikhokelo sichaza ukuba iziphumo ze-Sminofu Itekisi ku-YouTube ziqulathe ntoni ngokwenene, indlela yokuqinisekisa itshaneli phambi kokuba ucofe, kunye neendlela ezilumkileyo zokuva icwecwe elipheleleyo.

Inguqulelo emfutshane: i-albhamu iyinyani, inde, kwaye kufanelekile ukuyiva ngokulandelelana ubuncinane kube kanye. Inguqulelo ende ibandakanya ukwazi ukuba loluphi ulayisho olusemthethweni, loluphi oluyikopi ekhutshelweyo, nokuba ufumaneka phi umsasazo osemthethweni. Ukuba ukruqukile ziziphumo ezincinci okanye iividiyo eziyeka phakathi kwengoma, la macandelo angezantsi aza kukusindisa ixesha.

## Iingcebiso Ezisebenzayo Zokuyonwabela Ngokupheleleyo i-Albhamu

- **Mamela ngokulandelelana kube kanye.** Isingeniso simizuzu engu-1:28 kwaye silungiselela ngqo ingoma enegama elifanayo. Ukuxuba kuyilahla loo nto.

- **Gcina iingoma ezinde zohambo.** U-"Igeja" no-"Iyeke Leyondaba" zezona zimbini zithatha ixesha elininzi.
- **Jonga iikhredithi kwiphepha elisemthethweni.** Abaculi abadlalayo kulula ukubaphosa kulayisho lwevidiyo olususa imetadata.
- **Khuphela okanye gcina ukuze udlale ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.** Ulayisho lwe-albhamu kumaqonga evidiyo lunokunyamalala ngaphandle kwesilumkiso; uluhlu lweengoma olugciniweyo alunakwenza njalo.
- **Jonga umhla wokulayisha.** Nantoni na efakwe phambi kwe-16 Oktobha 2026 ayisiyo le albhamu, nokuba isihloko sithini.

Kubafundi abakhetha imifanekiso, iividiyo zamazwi eengoma ziyindlela ephakathi — kodwa thatha iaudio njengomboniso kwaye uqinisekise umxube wokwenene kwiqonga elisemthethweni emva koko.

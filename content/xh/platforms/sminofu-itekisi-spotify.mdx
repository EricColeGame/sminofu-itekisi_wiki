## Indlela Yokudlala i-Sminofu Itekisi ku-Spotify

2. **Gcina i-albhamu kwilayibrari yakho.** Ukucinezela intliziyo okanye uphawu lokongeza kugcina icwecwe kuqokelelo lwakho ukuze livele kwi-feed yasekhaya nakwizishwankathelo zokuphela konyaka.
3. **Khuphela ukuze uyilalele ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.** Ababhalisile be-Spotify Premium banokugcina zonke iingoma ezili-17 apha ekuhlaleni. [Isikhokelo esisemthethweni se-Spotify sokumamela ngaphandle kwe-intanethi](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/listen-offline/) sichaza indlela ukukhuphela okusebenza ngayo kwezinye iinkqubo.
